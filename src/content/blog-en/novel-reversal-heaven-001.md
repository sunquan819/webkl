## Chapter 1: The Boy in the Ruins

Deep within the vast mountain range, there lies an abandoned village.

The stone tablet at the village entrance had long shattered. Three characters "Tianyuan Village" could barely be seen on it. But no one knew why this village was abandoned, and no one remembered who once lived here.

In the wind and snow, a thin boy knelt among the ruins.

His clothes were torn, his face covered with frostbite, his hands gripping a rusted iron sword. This sword was dug from the ruins—his only possession.

The boy was named Ye Chen, sixteen years old.

Three days ago, his adoptive father threw him here. The adoptive father said: "Your aptitude is too poor, you cannot cultivate. Keeping you will only burden the family. Survive on your own."

Ye Chen didn't cry. He just knelt in the ruins, watching the direction his adoptive father left, until snow covered that road.

He knew his situation clearly.

On the Tianyuan Continent, cultivation is supreme. Mortals are like ants, cultivators are celestial beings. Those who cannot cultivate are destined to be stepped on for their entire lives.

Ye Chen had tried cultivating before.

At age seven, he was tested and found to have "Blocked Meridians"—his meridians were obstructed, unable to gather qi. His adoptive father took him in, hoping to find a way to clear his meridians. But after nine years, all attempts failed.

The adoptive father finally abandoned him.

Now, Ye Chen knelt in the abandoned ancestral village, before a decrepit ancestral hall.

Inside the hall, ancestral tablets of Tianyuan Village were enshrined. The characters on the tablets were blurred, but Ye Chen could vaguely see a line: "Those who defy heaven live; those who submit to heaven die."

This was the ancestral teaching of Tianyuan Village.

Ye Chen stared at that line for a long time.

"Those who defy heaven live; those who submit to heaven die."

He suddenly laughed.

A cold laugh.

"I have no aptitude, cannot cultivate. This is the fate heaven gave me," Ye Chen whispered. "But if I accept this fate, I am submitting to heaven. Those who submit to heaven die."

He gripped the iron sword tighter.

"So I cannot submit. I must defy heaven."

The snow intensified.

Ye Chen stood up and walked into the ancestral hall. Inside, there was a dried-up well, its bottom dark and unfathomable.

Legend said this well was left by Tianyuan Village's ancestors, containing secrets. But no one knew what they were.

Ye Chen had heard from his adoptive father that Tianyuan Village's ancestors were once powerful cultivators. Later, they offended a major power, and the entire village was wiped out. Only this well and ancestral hall remained.

Ye Chen approached the well.

He hesitated, then jumped down.

The well was deep. Ye Chen fell for a long time before landing on a platform.

The platform was surrounded by stone walls carved with strange runes. These runes emitted faint blue light, illuminating the entire space.

At the center of the platform, there was a black stone.

The stone was carved with a pattern: a human figure holding a long sword, standing on a shattered path. That path extended toward the sky but was broken in the middle.

Below the figure, three characters were written: "Defying Heaven Path."

Ye Chen approached the stone.

He reached out and touched those three characters.

Suddenly, the stone began to tremble.

A cold force surged from the stone into Ye Chen's body. His meridians began to ache intensely, as if being torn apart.

Ye Chen gritted his teeth and didn't retreat.

He knew this was an opportunity.

Blocked meridians had prevented him from cultivating. But this force was forcibly breaking through those obstructions.

The pain lasted for a long time.

Ye Chen felt his body might collapse. But he still gripped the iron sword, standing firm.

Finally, the force stopped.

Ye Chen gasped heavily. He looked down at his palm and found a black imprint—the figure pattern from the Defying Heaven Path.

Simultaneously, in his meridians, a weak stream of qi began to flow.

That was spiritual qi.

Ye Chen closed his eyes, feeling that stream.

Very weak. But it definitely existed.

He could finally cultivate.

But Ye Chen knew this was only a beginning.

He looked up at the runes on the stone wall. The runes formed a passage:

"The Defying Heaven Path is not orthodox. Those who cultivate this path are rejected by heaven and earth. A life of tribulations, nine deaths for one life. But if successful, one can break heavenly law and become a true powerhouse."

"First step of this path: Condense Defying Qi. Use Defying Qi to rush through meridians, breaking the blocked meridian curse. Second step: Forge Defying Sword. Condense Defying Qi into a sword—when the sword forms, the path forms. Third step: Defying Heaven Tribulation. Pass three heavenly tribulations to successfully defy heaven."

Ye Chen finished reading this passage.

He remained silent for a long time.

The Defying Heaven Path—a life of tribulations, nine deaths for one life.

This was the ancestors' warning to descendants. Cultivating the Defying Heaven Path carried enormous cost.

But Ye Chen didn't hesitate.

"I've already been thrown into ruins, my life like an ant. Even if cultivating the Defying Heaven Path means nine deaths for one life, it's better than being stepped on forever."

He gripped the iron sword.

"I choose the Defying Heaven Path."

In the wind and snow, deep in the abandoned Tianyuan Village, a boy began his journey of defying heaven.

This path was destined to be treacherous.

But Ye Chen didn't care.

He only knew one thing: those who submit to heaven die, those who defy heaven live.

He chose to live.
