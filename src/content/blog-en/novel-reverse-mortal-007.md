## Chapter 7: Nether Abyss Trials

Three days after the trial, Lin Xuan and Su Qinghan prepared to enter the depths of Nether Abyss.

Night Shadow provided a map.

"This is the map of Nether Abyss depths from Dark Hall investigation. The secret of Reverse Mortals lies in the deepest part of the abyss."

Lin Xuan accepted the map.

"How deep is the deepest part?"

Night Shadow said.

"Three hundred fathoms deeper than the cave we visited last time. There are trials left by ancient Reverse Mortals there."

Su Qinghan looked at the map.

"Trials? What trials?"

Night Shadow said.

"Three trials: Will Trial, Power Trial, Fate Trial. Only through the trials can one master the power to reverse fate."

---

Nether Abyss entrance, cliff edge.

Lin Xuan and Su Qinghan stood at the cliff edge, ready to descend.

Night Shadow stood behind them.

"I can only take you to the entrance. Once inside, your lives depend on yourselves."

Lin Xuan nodded.

"Thank you for your help."

Night Shadow said.

"Three months later, I will continue helping you against the Sect Master."

She turned and disappeared into the forest.

---

The two climbed down ropes, descending to the bottom of Nether Abyss.

At the abyss bottom, black mist was thicker, spiritual energy chaotic.

Last time they only explored the cave's surface layer.

This time, they needed to enter deeper parts of the abyss.

---

After walking about an hour, a stone door appeared ahead.

The stone door was carved with runes.

The runes were ancient text, Lin Xuan couldn't understand.

But the Reverse Mortal Pearl vibrated, as if telling him the meaning of the runes.

Su Qinghan looked at the runes.

"Ancient Reverse Mortal text. Can you read it?"

Lin Xuan gripped the Reverse Mortal Pearl.

"The Pearl tells me... behind the stone door is the first trial: Will Trial."

---

The stone door opened automatically.

Behind the door was a dark space.

In the space, there was a stone platform.

On the platform lay a black stone tablet.

The tablet read:

"Will Trial: Face fear, hold firm to conviction."

---

Lin Xuan entered the space.

The space suddenly transformed.

In the darkness, a scene appeared.

It was the Su family massacre three hundred years ago.

Lin Xuan saw Elder Zhang leading disciples besieging the Su family.

Su family members were slaughtered, blood covering the ground.

---

In the scene, Lin Xuan himself appeared.

He was captured by Elder Zhang, about to be killed.

"You have no spiritual root, you are worthless. Killing you purifies the world." Elder Zhang sneered.

Lin Xuan felt fear.

The fear of death.

The fear of powerlessness.

---

The scene continued.

Lin Xuan was killed by Elder Zhang.

Blood flowed everywhere.

Lin Xuan felt the pain of death.

It was real pain.

---

The scene changed again.

Lin Xuan saw himself kneeling before Elder Zhang, begging.

"Please don't kill me... I will do anything..."

Elder Zhang sneered.

"Worthless. Kneeling and begging, even more worthless."

---

The scene changed a third time.

Lin Xuan saw himself crying and screaming, fearful and desperate.

"I don't want to die... I'm afraid..."

Elder Zhang sneered.

"Crying worthless. Kneeling worthless. Even more worthless."

---

The scene looped.

Lin Xuan being killed, kneeling and begging, crying in fear.

Each scene was his fear.

Each scene was his powerlessness.

---

The Will Trial tested conviction when facing fear.

Lin Xuan needed to hold firm to his conviction, not defeated by fear.

---

Lin Xuan gripped the Reverse Mortal Pearl.

The pearl vibrated, dark red light flickering.

The Reverse Mortal Pearl seemed to tell him:

"You don't cry, kneel, or beg. This is your conviction."

---

In the scene, Lin Xuan no longer felt fear.

He looked at Elder Zhang.

"I am not worthless. I have the Reverse Mortal Pearl. I can reverse fate."

Elder Zhang in the scene froze.

"Reverse Mortal Pearl? How does a worthless one have the Reverse Mortal Pearl?"

Lin Xuan gripped the pearl.

"The Reverse Mortal Pearl chose me because I have conviction. I don't cry, kneel, or beg."

---

The scene collapsed.

The dark space disappeared.

Light appeared on the stone tablet.

"Will Trial: Passed. Conviction firm, unafraid of fear."

---

The first trial completed.

Lin Xuan exited the space, returning outside the stone door.

Su Qinghan watched him.

"You passed?"

Lin Xuan nodded.

"The Will Trial tested conviction. I held firm to my conviction and passed the trial."

Su Qinghan watched him with complex eyes.

"Your conviction is strong. Don't cry, kneel, or beg... this is the core of Reverse Mortal Chronicles."

---

They continued forward.

After walking about thirty minutes, a second stone door appeared ahead.

The stone door was carved with runes.

The Reverse Mortal Pearl vibrated, telling Lin Xuan the meaning of the runes.

"Power Trial: Face challenge, prove power."

---

The stone door opened.

Behind the door was a battle space.

In the space, a beast appeared.

Three-level beast: Black Scale Dragon.

The Black Scale Dragon was massive, scales hard, power astonishing.

Ten times stronger than the two-level Black Bear.

---

The Power Trial tested combat ability.

Lin Xuan needed to prove he had sufficient power.

---

Lin Xuan gripped his iron sword, ready for battle.

The Reverse Mortal Pearl vibrated, dark red light flickering.

But the Pearl's power wasn't enough to face a three-level beast.

---

The Black Scale Dragon charged.

Lin Xuan met it head-on, iron sword thrusting at the dragon's throat.

But the dragon's scales were too hard, the iron sword couldn't penetrate.

The dragon's tail struck Lin Xuan, knocking him back several steps.

---

Lin Xuan felt the pressure.

Three-level beast power far exceeded two-level beasts.

If the battle continued, he might fail.

---

Su Qinghan joined the battle.

Ice blue long sword swung, cold energy attacking the Black Scale Dragon.

The dragon's scales were frozen by cold energy, speed slowing.

---

The two coordinated, Lin Xuan attacking, Su Qinghan assisting.

But the Black Scale Dragon's power remained too strong.

The battle continued for half an hour, both injured.

---

Lin Xuan gripped the Reverse Mortal Pearl.

"Reverse power... give me power..."

The Pearl vibrated, dark red light erupted.

More cracks appeared on the pearl's surface, the cost显现.

---

The cost of reversing power was pressure on all meridians.

Lin Xuan's meridians felt severe pain, but he grit his teeth and persisted.

The Reverse Mortal Pearl's power erupted, dark red light enveloping Lin Xuan.

---

Lin Xuan felt power increase.

His iron sword became sharper, his strength became powerful.

He met the Black Scale Dragon head-on, iron sword thrusting at the scales.

---

The iron sword penetrated the scales, piercing through the Black Scale Dragon's throat.

The dragon fell to the ground, twitched a few times, and stopped breathing.

---

The battle ended.

Lin Xuan and Su Qinghan stood beside the Black Scale Dragon's corpse.

Both injured, but alive.

---

The space collapsed.

Light appeared on the stone tablet.

"Power Trial: Passed. Power proven, challenge overcome."

After walking about thirty minutes, a third stone door appeared ahead.

The stone door was carved with runes.

The Reverse Mortal Pearl vibrated, telling Lin Xuan the meaning of the runes.

"Fate Trial: Face fate, reverse fate."

In the scene, Lin Xuan no longer accepted the failed future.
