## 2026 Summer Travel Guide: Hot Destinations & Budget Planning

Summer travel is among the TOP 10 search trends of 2026. Student graduation trips, family vacations, and couple honeymoon trips are driving demand surge.

This guide compiles global hot destinations, visa processing, and budget planning, covering 5,000-100,000 yuan different budget needs.

## I. Hot Destinations Recommendations

### 1. Japan (Most Popular)

**Search Heat:** ★★★★★

**Suitable For:** Students, couples, families, shopping enthusiasts

**Best Time:** July-August (avoid cherry blossom peak)

**Recommended Cities:**

**Tokyo (5-7 days)**
- Must-visit: Senso-ji Temple, Tokyo Tower, Disney, Akihabara, Ginza
- Special experiences: Anime pilgrimage, shopping paradise, food exploration
- Per-person budget: 8,000-15,000 yuan

**Osaka (4-5 days)**
- Must-visit: Osaka Castle, Universal Studios, Nara Park, Shinsaibashi
- Special experiences: Food capital, shopping discounts, kimono experience
- Per-person budget: 6,000-12,000 yuan

**Kyoto (3-4 days)**
- Must-visit: Kinkaku-ji, Kiyomizu-dera, Fushimi Inari Taisha, Gion
- Special experiences: Traditional culture, temple meditation, tea ceremony
- Per-person budget: 5,000-10,000 yuan

**Visa Processing:**
- Single-entry visa: 200-300 yuan, valid 90 days
- Three-year multiple-entry: 500-800 yuan, 30 days per stay
- Processing time: 7-10 working days
- Materials: Passport, photos, income proof, itinerary

**Money-Saving Tips:**
- Flights: Book 2-3 months early, price 3,000-5,000 yuan
- Accommodation: Guesthouses, capsule hotels, 200-500 yuan/night
- Transport: JR Pass, 7-day pass 1,500 yuan
- Food: Convenience stores, ramen shops, 50-100 yuan/meal

## III. Visa Processing Guide

### Asian Country Visas

**Japan Visa**
- Single-entry: Valid 90 days, 15 days stay
- Three-year multiple: 30 days per stay
- Materials: Passport, photos, income proof, itinerary
- Processing time: 7-10 working days
- Fee: 200-500 yuan

**Korea Visa**
- Single-entry: Valid 90 days, 90 days stay
- Jeju visa-free: 30 days stay
- Materials: Passport, photos, application form
- Processing time: 5-7 working days
- Fee: 150-250 yuan

**Thailand Visa**
- Visa on arrival: 15 days stay, fee 400 yuan
- E-visa: 60 days stay
- Visa-free policy: Possibly visa-free in 2026
- Processing time: Visa on arrival same day

### European Schengen Visa

**Schengen Visa**
- Applicable countries: 26 Schengen countries (France, Italy, Germany etc.)
- Valid: 90 days, 90 days per stay
- Materials: Passport, photos, income proof, itinerary, hotel booking, insurance
- Processing time: 15-20 working days
- Fee: 800-1,200 yuan

**Notes:**
- Apply 1-2 months early
- Apply at main destination country embassy
- Provide detailed itinerary
- Buy travel insurance

### USA Visa

**B2 Tourist Visa**
- Valid: 10 years, 6 months per stay
- Materials: Passport, photos, asset proof, employment proof
- Processing time: 5-7 working days after interview
- Fee: 1,100 yuan

**Interview Tips:**
- Answer questions honestly
- Show intent to return (job, family)
- Provide asset proof
- Avoid sensitive topics

## IV. Flight Booking Tips

### Best Booking Time

**International Flights**
- Book 2-3 months early, lowest prices
- Off-season booking, 30-50% cheaper than peak season
- Watch airline promotions (Double 11, 618)

**Domestic Flights**
- Book 1-2 months early
- Watch special deals (Friday, Saturday updates)
- Use comparison sites (Ctrip, Fliggy, Qunar)

### Money-Saving Tips

**Choose Budget Airlines**
- Asia: AirAsia, Spring Airlines
- Europe: Ryanair, EasyJet
- USA: Southwest, JetBlue

**Avoid Peak Times**
- Summer peak: Mid-July - Mid-August
- Choose late June or late August
- Avoid Friday, Sunday travel

**Use Miles Redemption**
- Airline membership programs
- Credit card points redemption
- Miles tickets 50% cheaper than cash tickets

## V. Accommodation Booking Guide

### Accommodation Types

**Hotels**
- Price: 300-1,500 yuan/night
- Pros: Good service, full facilities
- Cons: Expensive, lacks character

**Guesthouses (Airbnb)**
- Price: 200-800 yuan/night
- Pros: Good value, local experience
- Cons: Variable service quality

**Hostels**
- Price: 50-200 yuan/night
- Pros: Cheap, meet friends
- Cons: Less privacy, basic facilities

**Apartments**
- Price: 400-1,500 yuan/night
- Pros: Family-friendly, cooking possible
- Cons: No hotel services

### Booking Tips

**Book Early**
- Book 1-2 months early, lowest prices
- Off-season booking, even cheaper

**Comparison Sites**
- Booking.com, Agoda, Airbnb
- Hotels.com, Expedia
- Compare price, reviews, location

**Watch for Deals**
