ChatGPT has become the hottest money-making tool of 2026. From students to office workers, freelancers to business owners, everyone is using ChatGPT to generate income.

## Method 1: Content Creation Services

**Income Range:** 3,000-15,000 yuan/month

**Market Demand:**
Self-media, businesses, and e-commerce platforms need massive amounts of content. WeChat articles, product copy, marketing materials, and short video scripts are in high demand.

**Step-by-Step:**

1. **Determine Service Type**
   - WeChat articles: 500-1,500 yuan/article
   - Product copy: 200-800 yuan/article
   - Short video scripts: 300-1,000 yuan/article
   - Marketing copy: 500-2,000 yuan/article

2. **Find Clients**
   - Freelance platforms: Zhubajie, Fiverr, Upwork
   - Social media: Xiaohongshu, Douyin, Zhihu
   - Direct outreach: SMEs, e-commerce stores

3. **Create Content with ChatGPT**
   ```
   Prompt Template:
   "Write an article about [topic], style [formal/casual/professional], word count [800-1500 words], target audience [description], include [specific requirements]"
   ```

4. **Optimize Content**
   - Add personal insights (avoid AI traces)
   - Adjust tone and style
   - Check factual accuracy
   - Insert relevant examples

**Real Case:**
Student Zhang, a junior in college, takes 10-15 WeChat article orders per month, earning 6,000-9,000 yuan. Each article starts with a ChatGPT draft, optimized in 30 minutes.

**Beginner Tips:**
- Register on Zhubajie first, build positive reviews
- Start with lower pricing to get initial orders quickly
- Offer free trial writing to showcase quality

1. **Determine Translation Type**

3. **Human Review**

   - Ensure contextual accuracy
