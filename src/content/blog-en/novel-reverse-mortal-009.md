## Chapter 9: Awakening

After returning from the Nether Abyss, Lin Xuan's life changed drastically.

He practiced the techniques taught by the Reverse Mortal Pearl every day, and his strength grew rapidly. But the cost was obvious — his hair began turning white, a sign of rapidly draining life force.

Su Qinghan visited him every day, and their feelings grew quickly through battles shoulder to shoulder.

"Your hair..." Su Qinghan caressed his strands with pain.

"Don't worry." Lin Xuan held her hand. "As long as I can protect you, this price is nothing."

---

One day, while browsing ancient sect books, Lin Xuan discovered hidden text.

"Lin Yuanshan, traitor of Reverse Heaven Sect, executed thirty years ago for colluding with demonic forces. His wife died in childbirth while fleeing, infant's whereabouts unknown."

Lin Xuan's hand trembled violently.

Thirty years ago, his father Lin Yuanshan was discovered "colluding with demonic forces." But Xiao Wuchen's letter stated that his father had discovered the sect's secret — they were secretly collecting essence from people without spiritual roots.

His father wasn't a traitor, but a martyr.

Lin Xuan's blood boiled.

He continued reading and found more shocking secrets.

"Lin Yuanshan's wife, before death, entrusted the infant to a farming household. The infant was implanted with 'false spiritual root', number 89757."

False spiritual root — an artificially created fake spiritual root.

Lin Xuan finally understood why his meridians were different from ordinary people from birth, why the Reverse Mortal Pearl chose him.

He wasn't "a person with spiritual roots," but "a manufactured person without spiritual roots."

He was the last bloodline his father protected with his life.

---

Lin Xuan found the farming household that had taken him in.

"Child, you've finally come." The old man was already white-haired. "Your parents, before they died, entrusted you to me. They said someday you'd come back to take what belongs to you."

The old man gave Lin Xuan a ragged cloth bundle.

In the bundle was a blood-stained letter and a broken jade pendant.

The letter was from his father:

"My son Lin Xuan:

When you read this letter, I may already be dead.

I'm not afraid of death. I'm only afraid you can't live well.

The high authorities of Xuantian Sect are demons. They're practicing a forbidden technique requiring large amounts of essence from people without spiritual roots. I was one of their experimental subjects, and escaped by luck.

They won't let me go, and they won't let you go.

If you can read this letter, it means you've encountered the Reverse Mortal Pearl. That was left by my old friend Xiao Wuchen. It will help you, but it will also take your life.

My child, don't hate. Don't seek revenge. Living well is my greatest wish.

Father Lin Yuanshan, final words"

Lin Xuan finished the letter with tears streaming down his face.

But he couldn't turn back now.

---

After returning to the sect, Lin Xuan began investigating in secret.

He discovered that thirty years ago, twenty-three people without spiritual roots were secretly executed by the sect to practice a forbidden technique called "Soul Extraction Technique."

This technique could forcibly extract others' spiritual roots and transfer them to oneself.

The high authorities of Xuantian Sect were secretly practicing this technique.

Their strength surpassing other sects was because they had been rapidly improving through this method.

Lin Xuan recorded all this evidence,准备有一天公之于众。

But just then, the sect discovered his investigation.

"Lin Xuan, you have quite the audacity."

In the elder council chamber, Sect Master Xuanmingzi's face was ashen.

"What are you investigating?" The law enforcement elder demanded.

Lin Xuan replied calmly: "I'm investigating the truth of thirty years ago."

"Truth? Hmph!" Xuanmingzi sneered. "Lin Yuanshan colluded with demonic forces, evidence is conclusive. Are you, as his son, going to follow his path?"

"My father wasn't a traitor." Lin Xuan stared directly at the sect master. "He was framed by you."

"How presumptuous!" The law enforcement elder shouted. "Seize him!"

At that moment, a figure stepped in front of Lin Xuan.

It was Su Qinghan.

"Sect master, Lin Xuan saved my life." Her voice was cold and clear. "If you want to touch him, you must get past me first."

Xuanmingzi's pupils contracted.

Behind Su Qinghan was also Night Shadow from the Shadow Hall.

"What, are you betraying the sect?" Xuanmingzi said coldly.

Night Shadow replied indifferently: "We just don't want to kill innocents."

The situation was deadlocked.

Lin Xuan knew today couldn't end peacefully.

He took a deep breath, and the Reverse Mortal Pearl within him began to circulate.

"Since the sect won't give me the truth, then I'll take it myself."
