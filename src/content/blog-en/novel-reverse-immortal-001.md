## Chapter 1: Below the Heng Yue Sect

The Heng Yue Sect, situated within the Heng Yue Mountain Range of the State of Zhao.

The mountain terrain was steep, clouds and mist winding around, like an immortal realm. This was the largest cultivation sect within the State of Zhao, recruiting disciples once every three years.

Countless mortals dreamed of entering the Heng Yue Sect, becoming cultivators, achieving immortality.

Today, beneath the Heng Yue Sect, over a thousand youths had gathered.

They came from all over the State of Zhao, all for one purpose—testing for spiritual roots.

Only those with spiritual roots could cultivate. Those without spiritual roots were destined to remain mortals for their entire lives.

In a corner of the crowd, a thin youth stood. His clothes were ragged, his face showed stubbornness.

He was named Wang Lin, fifteen years old.

Wang Lin came from Wang Family Village in the State of Zhao. To participate in this test, he walked for three months, begging along the way, nearly starving to death.

Wang Lin knew his situation very clearly.

He had known since childhood that he was just a mortal. Without spiritual roots, he was destined to be like an ant for life.

But he still came.

"Follow and be mortal, reverse and become immortal."

This phrase, Wang Lin had seen in a tattered book. That book was picked up from a dead person, recording some basic knowledge about cultivation.

Wang Lin didn't understand what "reverse and become immortal" meant. But he knew, if he didn't come for the test, he was accepting fate. Accepting fate was following.

He didn't want to follow.

In the crowd, a youth wearing a green shirt approached.

He was a disciple of the Heng Yue Sect, responsible for testing spiritual roots.

"All people line up, come one by one to test spiritual roots." The youth said coldly.

Wang Lin stood at the back of the line.

People ahead tested one by one. Some detected spiritual roots and were ecstatic. Some detected no spiritual roots and collapsed crying.

A youth with four-attribute spiritual roots was selected. He excitedly knelt on the ground, thanking the immortal.

A youth without spiritual roots was rejected. He despairingly knelt on the ground, crying loudly.

Wang Lin watched all this, no expression on his face.

Finally, it was Wang Lin's turn.

The green-shirted youth took out a jade tablet, let Wang Lin place his hand on it.

Wang Lin reached out, touching the jade tablet.

The jade tablet had no reaction.

The green-shirted youth coldly looked at Wang Lin: "No spiritual roots. Rejected."

Wang Lin withdrew his hand.

He didn't cry, didn't kneel. He just looked at that youth, his eyes ice-cold.

The youth felt uncomfortable from Wang Lin's gaze, frowned and said: "You still won't leave?"

Wang Lin turned, departed.

He walked a few steps, suddenly stopped.

On the ground was a stone. The stone was very ordinary, gray, completely unremarkable.

Wang Lin picked up the stone, gripped it in his palm.

"Follow and be mortal, reverse and become immortal." He quietly recited this phrase.

He suddenly laughed.

A cold laugh.

"Wang Lin, in this life, I don't accept fate."

Wang Lin left beneath the Heng Yue Sect, walked toward the deep mountains.

He knew, he had no spiritual roots, couldn't cultivate. But he wouldn't accept it.

He didn't want to be a mortal for his entire life.

He didn't want to be stepped on by others for his entire life.

Wang Lin walked for a long time, came to a cliff edge.

Below the cliff was darkness, bottomless.

Wang Lin stood at the cliff edge, looking at the abyss.

"I have no spiritual roots, cannot cultivate. This is fate." Wang Lin whispered. "But if I accept fate, I am following. Follow and be mortal."

He gripped the stone tighter.

"I don't accept fate. I want to reverse."

Wang Lin closed his eyes, took a deep breath.

He jumped, leaped off the cliff.

Wind whistled. Wang Lin plummeted.

He felt he was about to die.

Just then, the stone in his hand suddenly vibrated.

A cold force surged from the stone into Wang Lin's body.

Wang Lin's meridians began severe pain.

That force forcibly rushed through his blocked meridians.

Wang Lin grit his teeth, didn't scream.

He knew, this was an opportunity.

Mortals without spiritual roots were because meridians were blocked, unable to gather qi. But this force was forcibly opening his meridians.

The pain lasted for a long time.

Wang Lin felt his body was about to collapse. But he still gripped that stone, deathly tight.

Finally, that force stopped.

Wang Lin landed on a platform.

The platform was surrounded by stone walls carved with strange runes. These runes emitted faint blue light.

At the center of the platform, there was a dried-up well.

Beside the well stood a stone tablet. On the tablet were carved several characters:

"Heaven Reverse Pearl, a heaven-defying object. Those who obtain this pearl, cultivate by reversing heaven. A lifetime of tribulations, nine deaths for one life. If successful, can reverse heavenly law, become true immortal."

Wang Lin looked at the stone tablet.

He looked down at the stone in his hand.

The gray shell on the stone fell off, revealing a black pearl inside.

The pearl was very small, only fingernail-sized. But on it was carved a pattern—a human figure, standing on a shattered road, that road extended toward the sky.

Wang Lin gripped the pearl, feeling the force surging within it.

In his meridians, a weak stream of qi began to flow.

That was spiritual qi.

Wang Lin closed his eyes, feeling that stream.

Very weak. But it definitely existed.

He could finally cultivate.

Wang Lin looked at the warning on the stone tablet:

"A lifetime of tribulations, nine deaths for one life."

He was silent for a long time.

Then he laughed.

A cold laugh.

"Wang Lin, I already have nothing. Even if nine deaths for one life, it's better than being a mortal for a lifetime."

He gripped the Heaven Reverse Pearl tightly.

"Follow and be mortal, reverse and become immortal. I choose reverse."
