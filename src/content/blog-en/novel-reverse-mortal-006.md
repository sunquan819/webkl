## Chapter 6: Judgment and Choice

The day after killing Elder Zhang, Lin Xuan and Su Qinghan were brought to the Xuantian Sect's Inner Gate Hall.

The Inner Gate Hall was larger and more solemn than the Outer Gate Hall. Stone pillars were carved with runes, and the floor was paved with jade. Seven Elders sat in the hall, wearing robes of different colors.

Lin Xuan and Su Qinghan stood in the center of the hall, surrounded by watching disciples.

---

"Lin Xuan, Su Qinghan, you killed Outer Gate Elder Zhang." The Sect Master seated in the center spoke. The Sect Master was about sixty years old, with a dignified face, deep eyes, and wore a golden robe.

Su Qinghan said coldly.

"Elder Zhang participated in the Su family massacre three hundred years ago. We took revenge."

The Sect Master fell silent.

"The Su family massacre... something from three hundred years ago..."

Another Elder said.

"You killed an Elder, violating sect rules. You must be punished."

Lin Xuan gripped the Reverse Mortal Pearl tightly.

"Elder Zhang participated in the Su family massacre, this is fact. Our revenge is justice."

The Sect Master looked at Lin Xuan.

"Justice? In the sect, rules are justice. You violated the rules, so you violated justice."

---

The trial began.

The Sect Master announced.

"Lin Xuan, Su Qinghan, killing an Elder is a serious crime. Punishment options: expulsion from the sect, ten years imprisonment, death penalty."

Su Qinghan said.

"Elder Zhang participated in the Su family massacre, killing innocents. We killed him to avenge the Su family. This is justice."

An Elder in a blue robe said.

"The Su family massacre was three hundred years ago. Although Elder Zhang participated, he became a Sect Elder. You killing him challenges the sect's authority."

Another Elder in a red robe said.

"Elder Zhang's crimes were serious. The Su family massacre was a tragedy. But killing an Elder violates rules. We need to weigh this."

---

The Elders argued.

Some Elders believed Lin Xuan and Su Qinghan were guilty and deserved death.

Some Elders believed Elder Zhang's crimes were serious, and Lin Xuan and Su Qinghan's revenge was justified.

The argument continued for an hour without reaching consensus.

The Sect Master remained silent.

"We need more evidence. Whether Elder Zhang participated in the Su family massacre needs verification."

---

Outside the hall, a voice came.

"I have evidence."

Everyone turned to see a woman in black walking into the hall.

Night Shade.

A Dark Hall disciple.

Night Shade walked into the hall and stood before the Sect Master.

"Sect Master, I have evidence that Elder Zhang participated in the Su family massacre."

She took out a document.

"This is the Dark Hall's investigation record. Elder Zhang led disciples to surround the Su family three hundred years ago, killing three hundred Su family members."

The Sect Master took the document and read it.

"Dark Hall records... Elder Zhang indeed participated in the Su family massacre..."

---

Night Shade continued.

"Sect Master, Elder Zhang's crimes were serious. Although Lin Xuan and Su Qinghan's revenge was justified, it violated sect rules."

She looked at Lin Xuan and Su Qinghan.

"I suggest: Lin Xuan and Su Qinghan should not receive the death penalty, but expulsion from the sect. But give them three months to prepare, letting them complete a mission before leaving the sect."

The Sect Master fell silent.

"A mission? What mission?"

Night Shade said.

"In the depths of Nether Abyss, there are secrets of ancient Reverse Mortal practitioners. The sect needs these secrets. Let Lin Xuan explore as a mission before expulsion."

---

Lin Xuan gripped the Reverse Mortal Pearl.

In the depths of Nether Abyss, there were secrets of Reverse Mortal practitioners.

This was exactly what he wanted to know.

The Sect Master looked at Lin Xuan.

"Lin Xuan, are you willing to accept the mission?"

Lin Xuan nodded.

"I accept."

The Sect Master announced.

"Lin Xuan, Su Qinghan, killing an Elder, the crime is established. Punishment: expulsion from the sect. But give you three months to complete the Nether Abyss mission. After three months, expelled from the sect."

---

The trial ended.

Lin Xuan and Su Qinghan left the hall and returned to the Outer Gate stone house.

Night Shade followed.

"Lin Xuan, Su Qinghan, I need to tell you the truth."

Su Qinghan looked at Night Shade.

"The truth?"

Night Shade said.

"Elder Zhang wasn't the only one who participated in the Su family massacre. Others in the sect also participated."

Lin Xuan's heart trembled.

"Others?"

Night Shade nodded.

"The Sect Master also participated."

---

Lin Xuan and Su Qinghan were shocked.

"The Sect Master?"

Night Shade nodded.

"Three hundred years ago, the Sect Master was an Outer Gate Elder, and he also participated in the Su family massacre."

Su Qinghan said coldly.

"The Sect Master is one of the masterminds behind the Su family massacre?"

Night Shade nodded.

"Yes. But he is now the Sect Master, with the greatest power. You cannot oppose him."

Lin Xuan gripped the Reverse Mortal Pearl.

"The Sect Master participated in the Su family massacre... He judged us, actually wanting to cover up the truth."

Night Shade said.

"Yes. The Sect Master judged you to control the situation. If you received the death penalty, the truth would be covered up. If you were expelled, the truth would also be covered up."

She looked at Lin Xuan.

"But if you survive, you have a chance to expose the truth."

---

Lin Xuan fell silent.

The Sect Master participated in the Su family massacre, this was a bigger conspiracy.

Su Qinghan said.

"Night Shade, why do you help us?"

Night Shade said.

"I am a Dark Hall disciple. The Dark Hall's mission is to investigate the sect's internal secrets. Elder Zhang and the Sect Master's crimes, I investigated for three hundred years."

She looked at Lin Xuan.

"Lin Xuan, you have the Reverse Mortal Pearl. The Reverse Mortal Pearl can reverse spiritual energy, opposing the Sect Master."

Lin Xuan gripped the Reverse Mortal Pearl.

"The Reverse Mortal Pearl against the Sect Master? The Sect Master's strength is ten times stronger than Elder Zhang."

Night Shade said.

"The Reverse Mortal Pearl's power is not just reversing spiritual energy. Reversing fate is its true ability."

---

The Reverse Mortal Pearl trembled, dark red light flickering.

More cracks appeared on the pearl's surface, as if responding to Night Shade's words.

Lin Xuan felt the power inside the pearl.

Reversing fate.

Changing the future.

This was the Reverse Mortal Pearl's true ability.

---

Night Shade continued.

"In the depths of Nether Abyss, there are secrets of ancient Reverse Mortal practitioners. These secrets can help you master the ability to reverse fate."

She looked at Lin Xuan.

"Three months is enough for you to explore the depths of Nether Abyss. After completing the mission, you master the ability to reverse fate, then oppose the Sect Master."

Lin Xuan fell silent.

Three months.

Explore the depths of Nether Abyss.

Master the ability to reverse fate.

Oppose the Sect Master.

This was the path of revenge.

---

Su Qinghan said.

"Night Shade, you help us because you also hate the Sect Master?"

Night Shade nodded.

"I am an orphan. The sect adopted me and trained me to be a Dark Hall disciple. But after discovering the sect's crimes, I began investigating. Three hundred years of investigation, I accumulated extensive evidence."

She looked at Lin Xuan.

"Lin Xuan, Su Qinghan, you are the opportunity to break the sect's cover-up. I help you because I need you to expose the truth."

---

Lin Xuan looked at Night Shade.

"Why didn't you expose the Sect Master's crimes during the trial?"

Night Shade said.

"The Sect Master has the greatest power. If I publicly exposed him, I would be killed by the Sect Master. I can only help you in secret."

She looked at Lin Xuan.

"The Nether Abyss mission is an opportunity the Sect Master gave you. But he doesn't know that the depths of Nether Abyss hold the secret of reversing fate. After completing the mission, you master the ability to reverse fate, then oppose the Sect Master."

---

Lin Xuan fell silent.

The Sect Master gave the mission, not knowing it would let him master the ability to reverse fate.

This was the Sect Master's mistake.

And Lin Xuan's opportunity.

---

Night Shade said.

"Lin Xuan, Su Qinghan, three months. You explore the depths of Nether Abyss, master the ability to reverse fate. Then expose the Sect Master's crimes, overthrow the Sect Master."

She looked at Lin Xuan.

"This is the path of revenge. Also an opportunity to change the sect."

---

Su Qinghan said.

"Overthrow the Sect Master? We're just Outer Gate disciples."

Night Shade said.

"Lin Xuan has the Reverse Mortal Pearl. The Reverse Mortal Pearl's power is enough to oppose the Sect Master. Su Qinghan has ice spiritual roots, strong combat ability. You two working together have a chance to oppose the Sect Master."

She looked at Lin Xuan.

"But you need to master the ability to reverse fate. In the depths of Nether Abyss, there are secrets of Reverse Mortal practitioners. These secrets are your weapons against the Sect Master."

---

Lin Xuan gripped the Reverse Mortal Pearl.

Three months.

Explore the depths of Nether Abyss.

Master the ability to reverse fate.

Oppose the Sect Master.

Expose the truth.

Overthrow the Sect Master.

This was the path of revenge.

Also an opportunity to change the sect.

---

Su Qinghan looked at him.

"Lin Xuan, are you willing to accept this path?"

Lin Xuan nodded.

"I am willing."

Su Qinghan said.

"I am also willing. This is the path of revenge. Also an opportunity to change the sect."

She looked at Night Shade.

"Night Shade, thank you for your help."

Night Shade nodded.

"Don't thank me. I help you because I need you to expose the truth."

She turned, preparing to leave.

"After three months, I will continue to help you."

---

Night Shade left the stone house, disappearing into the night.

Lin Xuan and Su Qinghan stood outside the stone house.

Su Qinghan looked at him.

"Lin Xuan, three months. We need to prepare."

Lin Xuan nodded.

"Yes. We need to prepare."

Su Qinghan said.

"The depths of Nether Abyss are very dangerous. We need more strength."

Lin Xuan gripped the Reverse Mortal Pearl.

"The Reverse Mortal Pearl's power is not enough. I need to master the ability to reverse fate."

---

Su Qinghan looked at him.

"Lin Xuan, I will accompany you to explore the depths of Nether Abyss."

Lin Xuan looked at her.

"You're willing to accompany me?"

Su Qinghan nodded.

"You avenged the Su family. I accompany you to explore Nether Abyss as gratitude."

She looked into the distance.

"Also, this is an opportunity to change the sect. Overthrow the Sect Master, end the sect's crimes."

---

Lin Xuan was moved.

Su Qinghan accompanying him to explore the depths of Nether Abyss.

This was gratitude.

Also friendship.

Perhaps even love.

---

Su Qinghan turned, looking at Lin Xuan.

"Lin Xuan, three months. We prepare, then explore the depths of Nether Abyss."

Lin Xuan nodded.

"Okay."

Su Qinghan turned and left the stone house.

"Tomorrow, we start preparing."

---

Lin Xuan stood alone outside the stone house, gripping the Reverse Mortal Pearl.

The pearl trembled, dark red light flickering.

The Reverse Mortal Pearl seemed to tell him:

"The ability to reverse fate lies in the depths of Nether Abyss."

"Cost: life-threatening danger, nine deaths one life."

"Power: reverse fate, change the future."

Lin Xuan accepted in silence.

He didn't cry, didn't kneel, didn't beg.

This was the core of the Reverse Mortal path.

---

Late at night, Lin Xuan lay on the stone bed, gripping the Reverse Mortal Pearl.

He knew that in three months, he needed to explore the depths of Nether Abyss.

Master the ability to reverse fate.

Oppose the Sect Master.

Expose the truth.

Overthrow the Sect Master.

This was the path of revenge.

Also an opportunity to change the sect.

---

The Reverse Mortal Pearl trembled, dark red light flickering.

More cracks appeared on the pearl's surface, as if telling Lin Xuan:

"The ability to reverse fate requires a huge price."

"Cost: life-threatening danger, entire body meridians reshaping, memory fragmentation."

"Power: reverse fate, change the future, foresee danger."

Lin Xuan accepted in silence.

He didn't cry, didn't kneel, didn't beg.

The Reverse Mortal path continues forward.
