## What are Health Tracking Rings?

Health tracking rings are smart wearable devices worn on the finger that continuously monitor user health data through built-in sensors.

Unlike smartwatches, rings are more compact and comfortable to wear, making 24/7 continuous wear possible.

**Core Monitoring Functions**:
- **Heart Rate & Heart Rate Variability (HRV)**: Continuous cardiac health monitoring
- **Blood Oxygen Saturation (SpO2)**: Monitoring respiratory function
- **Body Temperature**: Continuous basic body temperature tracking
- **Sleep Analysis**: Deep analysis of all sleep stages
- **Activity Tracking**: Steps, calories, standing time
- **Stress Index**: Evaluating stress levels through physiological signals
- **Menstrual Cycle Tracking** (female): Predicting ovulation and periods

## Health Ring vs Smartwatch: How to Choose?

### Choose Health Ring When

1. **Focused on sleep monitoring**: Rings are more comfortable to wear, sleep data is more accurate
2. **Need 24/7 continuous monitoring**: Rings don't affect daily activities
3. **Allergic to watch materials**: Rings typically use titanium or ceramic
4. **Pursuing minimalist design**: Rings are more discreet and aesthetically pleasing
5. **Limited budget**: Entry-level rings are more affordable

### Choose Smartwatch When

1. **Need screen interaction**: Viewing notifications, maps, payments
2. **Rich exercise modes**: Professional athletes need GPS, etc.
3. **Emergency help features**: Fall detection, ECG
4. **Need haptic feedback**: Alarm, notification reminders

## Key Specifications for Choosing Health Rings

### 1. Sensor Accuracy
Choose products backed by multiple clinical studies. Oura and Samsung's accuracy has been validated repeatedly.

### 2. Battery Life
At least 7-day battery is the baseline. RingConn's 30-day battery is ideal for travel.

### 3. Material & Comfort
Titanium is premium standard, weight under 5 grams is optimal.

### 4. App Experience
Data visualization, trend analysis, expert advice — App experience directly affects usage motivation.

### 5. Compatibility
Confirm compatibility with your phone system (iOS/Android) and existing health apps (Apple Health/Google Fit).
