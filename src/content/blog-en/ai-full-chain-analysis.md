## AI Full Chain Analysis: Complete Industry Chain

AI industry isn't single link, it's complete chain. From chip hardware to final application, each layer has different companies and investment opportunities.

---

## AI Industry Chain Structure

**Five Layers:**

1. **Foundation**: Chips, hardware, infrastructure
2. **Energy**: Power supply, datacenter electricity, clean energy
3. **Technology**: Algorithms, frameworks, large models
4. **Application**: Products, services, solutions
5. **Data**: Data collection, processing, services

---

## Layer 2: Energy (Power Supply)

### AI Datacenter Power Demand

- Single AI datacenter uses 100-500MW (equals 100,000 homes)
- NVIDIA H100 chip power: 700W per card
- Global AI datacenters consume 2% of global electricity

### Power Companies

**NextEra Energy (NEE)**
- Largest US clean energy company
- Solar + wind capacity #1
- Google, Microsoft datacenter supplier
- 2026 PE 25x, **AI + Energy allocation 10%**

**Duke Energy (DUK)**
- Southeast US power supplier
- North Carolina datacenter cluster (Apple, Facebook)
- Stable dividend 4%
- 2026 PE 18x, **Defensive allocation 5%**

**Vertiv Holdings (VRT)**
- Datacenter power infrastructure
- UPS systems, thermal management
- NVIDIA partner supplier
- 2026 PE 35x, **AI infrastructure 5%**

### Clean Energy + AI

**First Solar (FSLR)**
- Largest US solar manufacturer
- Google, Amazon datacenter solar
- 2026 PE 20x
