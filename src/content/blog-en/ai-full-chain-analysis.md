## AI Full Chain Analysis: Complete Industry Chain & Investment Targets

AI industry isn't single link, it's complete chain. From chip hardware to final application, each layer has different companies and investment opportunities.

Understanding full chain enables precise investment. This article outlines AI industry chain four layers, core companies, stock codes, investment logic.

---

## AI Industry Chain Structure

**Four Layers:**

1. **Foundation Layer**: Chips, hardware, infrastructure
2. **Technology Layer**: Algorithms, frameworks, large models
3. **Application Layer**: Products, services, solutions
4. **Data Layer**: Data collection, processing, services

Each layer interdependent, foundation supports technology, technology enables application, data spans whole process.
