## What is WiFi 7? Why Does It Matter?

WiFi 7 is the latest generation wireless network standard after WiFi 6E, also known as IEEE 802.11be. Compared to the previous generation WiFi 6, WiFi 7 brings revolutionary performance improvements:

**Core Technology Upgrades**:
- **320MHz channel bandwidth**: 2× that of WiFi 6, dramatically improving transmission speed
- **4096-QAM modulation**: 20% more efficient data transmission than WiFi 6's 1024-QAM
- **Multi-Link Operation (MLO)**: Simultaneous transmission across multiple bands, breaking single-link bottlenecks
- **Up to 46Gbps theoretical speed**: Nearly 5× that of WiFi 6E
- **Ultra-low latency**: Latency drops below 1ms, perfect for cloud gaming and VR applications

Simply put: WiFi 7 makes "gigabit bandwidth into the home" a reality.

## WiFi 7 vs WiFi 6: Is It Worth Upgrading?

### Situations to Upgrade to WiFi 7

1. **Own WiFi 7 devices**: Latest flagship phones and laptops already support WiFi 7
2. **Gigabit+ bandwidth**: Your broadband speed exceeds 500Mbps
3. **High-density device environment**: Over 20 devices simultaneously connected at home
4. **Professional application needs**: Cloud gaming, 8K streaming, AI computing
5. **Large home coverage**: Need Mesh system for whole-home coverage

### Situations to Keep WiFi 6

1. **Devices don't support WiFi 7**: Most IoT devices still use WiFi 6
2. **Lower bandwidth**: 100-300Mbps broadband, WiFi 6 is sufficient
3. **Limited budget**: WiFi 7 router prices are still 2-3× that of WiFi 6
4. **Small home single-point coverage**: One WiFi 6 router is enough
