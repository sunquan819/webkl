## Chapter 2: Forcibly Entering the Sect

Around the platform, the rune's blue light gradually faded.

Lin Xuan gripped the Reverse Mortal Pearl, feeling the spiritual qi flowing through his meridians. Very weak, but definitely there.

He looked up at the summit above Juebi Peak.

To cultivate, he must enter Xuantian Sect.

Lin Xuan began climbing.

Juebi Peak had almost no path. The rocks were steep, the wind strong. Lin Xuan used both hands and feet, inching upward.

Two hours later, he reached the peak's edge.

Three white-robed disciples stood there.

"You're that eliminated one?" One frowned. "How did you climb up?"

Lin Xuan didn't answer. He stood up from the cliff edge and walked toward them.

"I want to enter Xuantian Sect."

The white-robed disciple sneered: "You have no spiritual roots, how can you enter?"

"I have a method." Lin Xuan said.

The white-robed disciple shook his head: "Impossible. Those without spiritual roots can never cultivate. That's the iron law."

Lin Xuan looked at him.

"Iron laws can be broken."

The white-robed disciple frowned: "What are you talking about?"

Lin Xuan took out the Reverse Mortal Pearl. The pearl was gray, looked ordinary.

The white-robed disciple glanced at it: "What's this?"

"It lets me cultivate." Lin Xuan said.

The white-robed disciple scoffed: "A broken stone lets you cultivate? You're dreaming."

Lin Xuan withdrew the pearl.

"Take me to see an elder."

The white-robed disciple shook his head: "Elders won't see you. You have no spiritual roots, you're mortal. Mortals have no qualification to see elders."

Lin Xuan was silent.

Then he walked toward Xuantian Sect's gate.

The white-robed disciple blocked him: "Stop. Mortals cannot enter."

Lin Xuan stopped.

He looked at the white-robed disciple, eyes ice-cold.

"I'm entering."

The white-robed disciple frowned: "You dare intrude Xuantian Sect?"

Lin Xuan didn't speak.

He walked directly toward the gate.

The white-robed disciple reached out to block. Spiritual qi surged, trying to push Lin Xuan away.

But Lin Xuan didn't retreat.

The Reverse Mortal Pearl vibrated. A force surged from Lin Xuan's body, countering the white-robed disciple's spiritual qi.

The white-robed disciple paused.

"You... how do you have spiritual qi?"

Lin Xuan continued walking.

He entered Xuantian Sect's gate.

Behind him, the white-robed disciple shouted: "Mortal intruding the sect! Someone is intruding!"

Soon, over a dozen white-robed disciples arrived.

They surrounded Lin Xuan.

"You have no spiritual roots, cannot enter Xuantian Sect." One said. "Leave, or we throw you out."

Lin Xuan stood among them.

He was calm.

"I won't leave."

The white-robed disciple sneered: "Then we throw you out."

They attacked together. Over a dozen streams of spiritual qi rushed toward Lin Xuan.

Lin Xuan gripped the Reverse Mortal Pearl tighter.

The pearl vibrated. A force surged out, forming a barrier around his body.

Spiritual qi struck the barrier, didn't push him back.

The white-robed disciples froze.

"What... what is this?"

An elder appeared.

He wore gray robes, looked fifty. Cold eyes.

"What's happening?"

The white-robed disciple said: "Elder, this mortal intruded. He has no spiritual roots, but can counter our spiritual qi."

The elder looked at Lin Xuan.

He looked for a long time.

"Where did you come from?"

"Juebi Peak." Lin Xuan said.

The elder frowned: "Juebi Peak is forbidden territory. Why did you go there?"

"I jumped down." Lin Xuan said. "Those without spiritual roots can only accept fate. I don't accept fate, so I jumped."

The elder was silent.

"You survived?"

"Survived." Lin Xuan took out the Reverse Mortal Pearl. "And obtained this."

The elder looked at the pearl.

His eyes slightly widened.

"This is..."

He reached out, wanting to take the pearl.

Lin Xuan withdrew his hand.

"It's mine."

The elder frowned: "You, a mortal, holding this thing?"

"It lets me cultivate." Lin Xuan said. "I want to enter Xuantian Sect."

The elder sneered: "You think having a pearl lets you enter? Xuantian Sect doesn't accept mortals. That's the rule."

"Rules can be broken." Lin Xuan said.

The elder shook his head: "Impossible."

He waved his hand: "Throw him out."

The white-robed disciples attacked again.

This time they used stronger spiritual qi.

Lin Xuan grit his teeth. The Reverse Mortal Pearl vibrated. The barrier thickened, blocking the qi.

But he felt strained.

The spiritual qi in his meridians was almost depleted.

He was about to fail.

Just then, the Reverse Mortal Pearl vibrated again.

An ice-cold force surged out, forcibly replenishing the meridians.

Lin Xuan felt severe pain.

The cost was manifesting.

But he didn't fall.

He stood in place, the barrier didn't break.

The elder frowned.

"Your pearl... is special."

He was silent for a moment.

"Outer gate trial begins in three months. If you pass, you can enter outer gate."

The white-robed disciple froze: "Elder, he has no spiritual roots—"

The elder interrupted: "He has a special object. Let him try."

He looked at Lin Xuan: "Three months, outer gate trial. Pass, you enter outer gate. Fail, you leave forever."

Lin Xuan nodded.

"I will pass."

The elder turned and left.

The white-robed disciples sneered: "Mortal trial? You can't possibly pass. Trial requires spiritual qi, your qi is so weak, you'll die in three days."

Lin Xuan didn't answer.

He walked to Xuantian Sect's outer gate area.

Several crude houses stood there. For trial participants.

Lin Xuan entered one.

He sat down, gripped the Reverse Mortal Pearl.

In his meridians, spiritual qi flowed. The pearl was slowly changing his meridians, making them wider, stronger.

But each change brought severe pain.

Lin Xuan grit his teeth and endured.

This was the cost.

Lifetime tribulations, nine deaths for one life.

First tribulation, was beginning.
