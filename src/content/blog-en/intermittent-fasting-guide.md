## Intermittent Fasting Guide: Three Methods Explained

Intermittent Fasting (IF) isn't dieting, it's time management.

Not restricting what you eat, but when you eat. This makes weight loss simpler, no need counting calories, no need preparing special food.

Three mainstream methods: 16/8, 5:2, OMAD. Which suits you best? After reading this, you'll know.

---

## Intermittent Fasting Principles

Why fasting works?

**Principle 1: Insulin Drops**
After eating, insulin rises, body stores fat. During fasting, insulin drops, body burns fat.

**Principle 2: Autophagy Starts**
After 12 hours fasting, body starts autophagy mechanism, cleans damaged cells, repairs tissues.

**Principle 3: Metabolic Switch**
Fasting makes body switch from burning sugar to burning fat, fat burning efficiency increases.

This isn't theory, it's mechanisms supported by scientific research.

## Method 1: 16/8 (Most Beginner-Friendly)

### What It Is?

Daily 16 hours fasting, 8 hours eating.

Like:
- Eating window: 12:00-20:00 (noon 12pm to evening 8pm)
- Fasting window: 20:00-next day 12:00 (evening 8pm to next day noon 12pm)

Within 8 hours eat two or three meals, 16 hours don't eat.

### Why Suits Beginners?

16/8 suits beginners because:
- Moderate fasting time (16 hours not hard)
- Flexible eating window (can adjust time)
- Daily social life unaffected (dinner with friends possible)
- No need major diet habit changes

### How To Execute?

**Step 1: Choose Eating Window**
Based on lifestyle choose. Common choices:
- 10:00-18:00 (breakfast type)
- 12:00-20:00 (lunch type)
- 14:00-22:00 (dinner type)

Suggest choosing 12:00-20:00, because lunch dinner social opportunities more.

**Step 2: Normal Diet Within Eating Window**
Within 8 hours, normal eating. No need dieting, no need counting calories.

But note:
- Don't binge eat
- Avoid high sugar high oil foods
- Eat more protein and vegetables

**Step 3: Only Water During Fasting Window**
Within 16 hours, only water, black coffee, tea (no sugar no milk).

Don't eat anything with calories.

**Step 4: Gradual Progress**
Week 1: 12 hours fasting (like 20:00-next day 8:00)
Week 2: 14 hours fasting (like 20:00-next day 10:00)
Week 3: 16 hours fasting (like 20:00-next day 12:00)

Gradual progress, body adapts faster.

### Important Notes

16/8 important notes:
- Morning might have hunger feeling, drink black coffee relieve
- During fasting maintain hydration, daily at least 2 liters water
- Exercise arrange in eating window or late fasting period
- Stop eating 2 hours before sleep (digestion time)

## Method 2: 5:2 (Suits Office Workers)

### What It Is?

Weekly normal eating 5 days, fasting 2 days.

Fasting days not complete no eating, but restricting calorie intake (500-600 calories).

Like:
- Monday to Friday: normal diet
- Saturday: fasting day (500 calories)
- Sunday: fasting day (500 calories)

### Why Suits Office Workers?

5:2 suits office workers because:
- Workdays normal diet, doesn't affect work
- Weekend fasting, time flexible
- Fasting days can arrange light activities
- No need daily counting time

### How To Execute?

**Step 1: Choose Fasting Days**
Choose low pressure, few activities days. Suggest weekend two days.

**Step 2: Fasting Day Diet**
How to distribute 500-600 calories?
- Breakfast: 200 calories (like two eggs + one slice whole wheat bread)
- Lunch: skip
- Dinner: 300 calories (like vegetable salad + small amount protein)

Or:
- Breakfast: skip
- Lunch: 250 calories (like vegetable soup + small amount chicken)
- Dinner: 250 calories (like steamed fish + vegetables)

**Step 3: Normal Day Diet**
Normal days no need dieting, but avoid binge eating. Maintain healthy diet habits.

**Step 4: Fasting Day Arrangement**
Fasting days arrange light activities:
- Read books, watch movies, meditate
- Don't do high intensity exercise
- Early sleep early rise

### Important Notes

5:2 important notes:
- Fasting days might have fatigue feeling, reduce workload
- Maintain hydration, daily at least 2 liters water
- Two consecutive fasting days difficult, can separate (like Tuesday and Saturday)
- Fasting days avoid social activities

## Method 3: OMAD (One Meal A Day)

### What It Is?

OMAD (One Meal A Day), daily only eat one meal.

Fasting 23 hours, eating 1 hour.

Like:
- Eating window: 18:00-19:00 (evening 6pm to 7pm)
- Fasting window: 19:00-next day 18:00

Within 1 hour finish whole day's meal.

### Why Suits Advanced Practitioners?

OMAD suits advanced practitioners because:
- Long fasting time (23 hours)
- Needs strong willpower
- Needs nutrition knowledge (one meal intake sufficient nutrition)
- Not everyone suits

But benefits obvious:
- Fastest weight loss effect
- Strongest autophagy effect
- Most time saved (no need prepare three meals)

### How To Execute?

**Step 1: Choose Eating Time**
Suggest choosing dinner time (18:00-19:00), because:
- Dinner social opportunities more
- After dinner can relax
- Before sleep won't hungry

**Step 2: One Meal Intake Sufficient Nutrition**
One meal needs contain:
- Protein: 30-50 grams (chicken, fish, beef, tofu)
- Vegetables: large amount (at least 500 grams)
- Quality fat: olive oil, nuts, fish oil
- Carbohydrates: moderate (whole grains, root vegetables)

Total calories suggest: 1500-2000 calories (based on weight and activity level).

**Step 3: Eating Window Control**
Within 1 hour finish eating. Don't eat too fast, chew slowly.

**Step 4: Maintain Hydration During Fasting**
23 hours fasting, maintain hydration. Daily at least 2 liters water.

### Important Notes

OMAD important notes:
- First week might very hungry, persist one week then adapt
- One meal can't binge eat, easy indigestion
- Need ensure nutrition sufficient, suggest supplement vitamins
- Not suits pregnant women, children, elderly
- Not suits diabetes patients, stomach disease patients

## Three Methods Comparison

| Method | Fasting Time | Eating Time | Suitable People | Difficulty |
|--------|-------------|-------------|----------------|-----------|
| 16/8 | 16 hours | 8 hours | Beginners, social active people | Low |
| 5/2 | 2 days/week | 5 days/week | Office workers, weekend flexible people | Medium |
| OMAD | 23 hours | 1 hour | Advanced practitioners, strong willpower people | High |

Selection suggestions:
- Beginners: Start from 16/8 method
- Office workers: 5:2 method more flexible
- Want fast weight loss: Try OMAD method (consult doctor first)

## Intermittent Fasting Risks

Not suits everyone:

**Unsuitable People:**
- Pregnant women, breastfeeding women
- Children, adolescents
- Elderly (over 65)
- Diabetes patients (need doctor guidance)
- Stomach disease patients (gastritis, ulcers)
- Underweight people (BMI<18.5)
- Eating disorder patients

**Potential Risks:**
- Initial hunger feeling strong
- Initial fatigue, attention drop
- Might lead nutrition deficiency (need ensure nutrition intake)
- Might lead eating disorder (overly focus eating time)

**Suggestions:**
Before trying consult doctor, especially people with chronic diseases.
