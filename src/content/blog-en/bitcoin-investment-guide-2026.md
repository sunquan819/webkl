## II. Investment Strategy Analysis

### Strategy 1: Long-term Holding (HODL)

**Suitable For:**
- Long-term investors (5-10 years)
- Believing in Bitcoin value
- Strong risk tolerance

**How to Execute:**

1. **Regular Buying (DCA)**
   - Buy fixed amount monthly (e.g., 5,000 yuan/month)
   - Ignore short-term volatility
   - Accumulate holdings long-term

2. **Hold Without Selling**
   - Don't sell for 5-10 years after buying
   - Avoid emotional decisions
   - Wait for major price appreciation

3. **Stop-loss & Take-profit**
   - No stop-loss (faith-based holding)
   - Take-profit: Sell after 10 years as needed

**Advantages:**
- Simple to execute
- Long-term returns substantial (historical annualized return 30-50%)
- Avoid frequent trading mistakes

**Risks:**
- Short-term volatility intense (may drop 30-50%)
- Long-term uncertainty (regulatory, technical risks)
- Requires long-term capital (5-10 years)

**Real Case:**
Mr. Zhang, bought 10 Bitcoins in 2015 ($300 each, total $3,000), held until 2026, value $700,000, annualized return ~60%.

---

### Strategy 2: Short-term Trading

**Suitable For:**
- Professional traders
- Strong risk tolerance
- Have time to monitor market

**How to Execute:**

1. **Technical Analysis**
   - Learn candlestick charts, MACD, RSI indicators
   - Watch support levels, resistance levels
   - Determine buy/sell timing

2. **Position Control**
   - Single trade no more than 20% of total capital
   - Buy/sell in batches
   - Avoid all-in operations

3. **Stop-loss & Take-profit**
   - Stop-loss: Stop when down 5-10%
   - Take-profit: Take when up 10-20%
   - Strictly follow discipline

**Advantages:**
- Short-term returns substantial (possibly 10-50%)
- Flexibly respond to market changes
- Quickly accumulate experience

**Risks:**
- Volatility intense, easy to lose
- Requires professional skills and time
- Emotional decisions lead to mistakes
- Trading fees consume returns

**Real Case:**
Mr. Li, professional trader, short-term traded Bitcoin March-May 2026, gained 50%. But needs monitoring market 6-8 hours daily, high risk pressure.

## IV. Risk Control Strategies

### Risk 1: Price Volatility Risk

**Risk Description:**
Bitcoin price volatility intense, may have 10-20% daily volatility, 30-50% monthly volatility.

**Control Methods:**

1. **Position Control**
   - Bitcoin position no more than 20% of total assets
   - Buy in batches, avoid all-in at once
   - Keep cash for pullback

2. **Stop-loss Strategy**
   - Set stop-loss level (e.g., stop at 10% down)
   - Execute stop-loss, avoid emotional decisions
   - Short-term trading must stop-loss

3. **Psychological Preparation**
   - Accept volatility as normal
   - Long-term holding ignore short-term volatility
   - Avoid panic selling

---

### Risk 2: Exchange Risk

**Risk Description:**
Exchanges may collapse, be hacked, regulatory shutdown (like FTX event).

**Control Methods:**

1. **Choose Reliable Exchange**
   - Top exchanges: Binance, Coinbase, Kraken
   - Avoid small, new exchanges
   - Check exchange security record

2. **Distributed Storage**
   - Don't put all Bitcoin on exchange
   - Large Bitcoin transfer to personal wallet
   - Exchange only keep small trading funds

3. **Use Cold Wallet**
   - Hardware wallet: Ledger, Trezor
   - Offline storage, hackers cannot attack
   - Backup private key, seed phrase

---

### Risk 3: Regulatory Risk

**Risk Description:**
Various countries regulatory policy changes, may ban Bitcoin trading, mining, holding.

**Control Methods:**

1. **Watch Regulatory Dynamics**
   - Watch US, EU, China regulatory policies
   - Understand compliant exchanges, compliant channels
   - Avoid illegal operations

2. **Use Compliant Channels**
   - Invest through compliant ETF funds
   - Use compliant exchanges (Coinbase, Kraken)
   - Follow local laws and regulations

3. **Diversify Risks**
   - Don't put all assets in Bitcoin
   - Keep traditional investments (stocks, bonds)
   - Avoid single asset risk

---

### Risk 4: Technical Risk

**Risk Description:**
Bitcoin technical vulnerabilities, quantum computing threat, network attacks.

**Control Methods:**

1. **Understand Technical Principles**
   - Learn Bitcoin basic technology
   - Understand private key, seed phrase importance
   - Avoid technical mistakes

2. **Use Security Tools**
   - Hardware wallet store large Bitcoin
   - Backup private key, seed phrase (multiple backups)
   - Regularly check wallet security

3. **Watch Technical Development**
   - Watch Bitcoin technical upgrades
   - Understand quantum computing threat solutions
   - Keep technical sensitivity

---

### Risk 5: Fraud Risk

**Risk Description:**
Cryptocurrency field fraud frequent (phishing websites, fake exchanges, Ponzi schemes).

**Control Methods:**

1. **Identify Fraud**
   - Don't believe high return promises (DeFi annualized >20% needs caution)
   - Don't click strange links
   - Don't believe "inside information"

2. **Use Official Channels**
   - Download wallet from official website
   - Use official exchange links
   - Verify address authenticity

3. **Protect Privacy**
   - Don't reveal Bitcoin holding amount
   - Don't public wallet address
   - Avoid social media showing off
