## Chapter 1: The Mortal Boy's Choice

Beneath the Xuantian Sect, over a thousand youths had gathered.

The Xuantian Sect was the largest cultivation sect within the Cloud Kingdom, recruiting disciples once every three years. Countless mortals dreamed of becoming Xuantian Sect disciples, stepping onto the cultivation path.

Cultivators could achieve immortality. Mortals died within a hundred years.

This was the difference.

In the center of the crowd, a white-robed Xuantian Sect disciple took out a testing disk, saying coldly: "Everyone line up, test spiritual roots one by one. Only those with spiritual roots can cultivate. Those without spiritual roots will forever remain mortals."

The line was very long.

Lin Xuan stood at the back, appearing very thin. He was sixteen, from Lin Family Village in the Cloud Kingdom. To test spiritual roots at Xuantian Sect, he walked for two months, nearly starving to death.

Youths ahead tested one by one.

Someone detected three-attribute spiritual roots, excitedly kneeling to thank.

Someone detected no spiritual roots, despairing and crying.

Lin Xuan watched all this, expressionless.

He knew his situation clearly. Mortals in this cultivation world were like ants. Without spiritual roots, forever stepped on by others.

Finally, Lin Xuan's turn.

The white-robed disciple let Lin Xuan place his hand on the testing disk.

Lin Xuan reached out.

The testing disk had no reaction.

The white-robed disciple coldly said: "No spiritual roots. Eliminated."

Lin Xuan withdrew his hand, turned to leave.

He didn't cry, didn't kneel, didn't beg. He just looked at that white-robed disciple, eyes ice-cold.

The white-robed disciple frowned: "You still won't leave?"

Lin Xuan didn't answer, continued walking.

After ten steps, he stopped.

Lin Xuan raised his head to look at Xuantian Sect's summit. There, clouds and mist wound around, the world of cultivators. And he, beneath his feet was dirt, the world of mortals.

Lin Xuan took out a black stone from his chest. This stone was picked up from a dead person, he didn't know what use it had.

He gripped the stone, whispered: "Mortals die within a hundred years. Cultivators can achieve immortality. This is the rule set by heavenly law. But I, Lin Xuan, don't want to die."

He laughed.

A cold laugh.

"If I accept fate, I am a mortal. Mortals die in a hundred years. If I don't accept fate, I must reverse this rule. Those who reverse rules may die sooner. But at least I tried."

Lin Xuan turned, walked toward Xuantian Sect's rear mountain—Juebi Peak.

Juebi Peak was Xuantian Sect's forbidden territory, legend said many died there.

Lin Xuan walked to Juebi Peak's edge, looking at the abyss below.

"No spiritual roots, cannot cultivate. This is heavenly law's rule." Lin Xuan whispered. "If I accept this rule, I am a mortal. But I don't accept."

He gripped the black stone tighter.

"I, Lin Xuan, in this life, don't accept fate."

He jumped down.

Wind whistled, Lin Xuan plummeted.

Just as he was about to crash into the ground, the black stone in his hand suddenly vibrated.

A strange force surged from the stone into Lin Xuan's body.

His meridians ached intensely. That force forcibly rushed through his blocked meridians.

Lin Xuan grit his teeth, didn't scream.

Mortals without spiritual roots were because meridians were blocked, unable to gather qi. But this force was forcibly opening his meridians.

The pain lasted a long time.

Lin Xuan felt he was about to die. But he still gripped that stone.

Finally, he landed on a platform.

The platform's surroundings were carved full of runes, emitting faint blue light.

At the platform's center was a stone tablet. On the tablet was written:

"This stone is named Reverse Mortal Stone. Those who obtain this stone can reverse heavenly law's rules, forcibly cultivating with a mortal body. Cost: a lifetime of tribulations, nine deaths for one life. If successful, can transcend heavenly law's rules, achieve true immortal."

Lin Xuan looked at the stone tablet.

He looked down at the stone in his hand. The black shell on the stone fell off, revealing a gray pearl inside.

On the pearl was carved a pattern: a human figure standing on a shattered road, that road extended toward the sky.

Lin Xuan gripped the pearl, feeling the force surging within it.

In his meridians, a weak stream of qi began to flow.

That was spiritual qi.

Lin Xuan closed his eyes to feel.

Very weak, but it definitely existed.

He could finally cultivate.

But the cost was "a lifetime of tribulations, nine deaths for one life."

Lin Xuan was silent for a long time.

Then he laughed.

A cold laugh.

"I, Lin Xuan, already have nothing. Even if nine deaths for one life, it's better than dying silently after a hundred years."

He gripped the Reverse Mortal Stone tighter.

"Mortals die in a hundred years. I don't accept fate. I will reverse this rule."
