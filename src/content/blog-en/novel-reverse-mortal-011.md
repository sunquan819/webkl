## Chapter 11: Abyss of the Dead

Three days later, deep in the mountains.

Lin Xuan stood before a massive abyss, black mist rising from its depths carrying a stench of decay and death.

The Abyss of the Dead.

Legend said this place buried the bones of countless powerful beings from ancient times — one of the most dangerous forbidden zones in the cultivation world.

"Are you sure you want to go in?" Su Qinghan stood beside him, her expression grave. "Legend says ancient fierce beasts are trapped here, along with all manner of诡异的 traps. Of those who enter, barely one in ten survives."

"No gain without risk." Lin Xuan said. "The information Xiao Wuchen left behind mentioned an ancient ruin deep within the Abyss. It may contain secrets about the Soul Extraction technique."

"Even with danger, it's worth trying." Night Shadow said. "The Xuantian Sect's kill order has reached the entire cultivation world. We need to grow stronger quickly."

Lin Xuan nodded.

The three of them leapt into the abyss.

---

Inside the abyss, black mist filled everything, visibility near zero.

Lin Xuan activated his true essence, forming a faint light barrier around himself, barely illuminating a few dozen feet.

Beneath their feet were piles of white bones.

Countless people had died here.

"Careful." Night Shadow suddenly spoke.

Before his words finished, a black shadow burst from the mist, lunging straight for Lin Xuan's face.

Lin Xuan dodged sideways while slashing out with his sword.

Sword light cut through the darkness, striking the shadow.

"Squeak—!" A mournful shriek as the shadow was sliced in two, falling to the ground.

It was a black bat, but ten times the normal size, with two fangs gleaming with eerie green light.

"Soul-Devouring Bats." Su Qinghan said. "Evil creatures that feed on corpses. They usually travel in swarms."

As her voice faded, a rustling sound echoed from the surrounding mist.

Countless pairs of ghostly green eyes lit up in the darkness.

"Run!" Lin Xuan shouted.

The three sprinted deeper into the abyss, behind them a tide of Soul-Devouring Bats surging forward.

---

After an unknown time running, they finally shook off the bat swarm's pursuit.

They arrived at a vast underground space.

Pale blue light filled the space, illuminating their surroundings.

"This is..." Lin Xuan's pupils constricted.

Before them stood a massive stone gate.

Ancient runes covered the gate, emanating a mysterious aura.

"An ancient ruin." Night Shadow said. "Looks like we found the right place."

Lin Xuan approached, carefully observing the runes on the gate.

These runes seemed to be some kind of seal, requiring a specific method to open.

He took out a jade slip from his怀中, containing information Xiao Wuchen left behind.

"A drop of heart blood is needed." He said. "Ancient cultivators' seals require blood as the key."

With that, he bit his finger, letting a drop of blood fall onto the stone gate.

The blood seeped into the runes, and the gate slowly opened.

---

Beyond the gate was a long corridor.

Both walls were carved with murals depicting the history of ancient times.

Lin Xuan walked along, studying them, his heart growing more shocked with each passing moment.

These murals recorded the origin of the Soul Extraction technique!

"In ancient times, the cultivation world suffered a great catastrophe." He murmured. "That disaster nearly destroyed the entire cultivation world."

The murals depicted: In ancient times, an evil cultivator called "Soul-Devouring Demon Ancestor" created the Soul Extraction technique, a cultivation method that could absorb others' life force to strengthen oneself. Relying on this technique, the Demon Ancestor nearly dominated the entire cultivation world.

Later, all major sects of the cultivation world united, and after a brutal war, finally defeated the Demon Ancestor, sealing him deep within the Abyss of the Dead.

But the secrets of the Soul Extraction technique did not disappear — some preserved it in secret.

"So the Xuantian Sect..." Su Qinghan's voice trembled. "They possess the evil arts of an ancient demon ancestor?"

"It's more than that." Lin Xuan's expression was grave. "The murals say the Soul-Devouring Demon Ancestor didn't truly die. His soul was sealed deep within the Abyss, capable of awakening at any time. The true purpose of the Xuantian Sect's Soul Extraction technique may not be cultivation, but—"

He paused.

"Awakening the Soul-Devouring Demon Ancestor."

At the corridor's end stood a massive altar.

On the altar lay a yellowed ancient book, three characters on its cover striking —

**"Demon Slaying Record"**

Lin Xuan approached and picked up the ancient book.

"This is..." He翻开第一页, his eyes brightening. "A technique to counter the Soul Extraction technique!"

"This book details the principles of Soul Extraction and how to counter it." Lin Xuan said. "If we can cultivate this technique, the Xuantian Sect's Soul Extraction will no longer threaten us."

"Excellent!" Night Shadow said. "Shall we cultivate here?"

"No." Lin Xuan shook his head. "It's too dangerous here. Let's leave the Abyss first and find a safe place to cultivate."

He tucked the "Demon Slaying Record" into his怀中 and turned toward the corridor exit.

At that moment, a cold voice rang out from behind.

"Want to leave? Not so easy."

Lin Xuan spun around.

At the corridor entrance stood a black-clad figure.

An elderly man in black robes, his face withered, eyes sunken but glowing with an eerie red light.

"Xuantian Sect's Enforcement Elder?" Su Qinghan's face changed dramatically. "How is he here?"

"Tracking talisman." Night Shadow gritted his teeth. "They left tracking talismans on us!"

"Brat, did you think escaping the sect meant safety?" His voice was cold and sharp. "This Abyss of the Dead is my territory. You entered, so there's no leaving alive."

Lin Xuan's eyes narrowed.

This elder's cultivation, at least Golden Core late stage, was a full major realm above his current Foundation Establishment mid stage.

"Lin Xuan, take them and go." Night Shadow suddenly said. "I'll hold him off."

"No way!" Su Qinghan said. "You alone can't defeat him!"

"Calm down." Lin Xuan said firmly. "The three of us together, we might still win."

He looked at Night Shadow and Su Qinghan, both waiting for his orders.

The Reverse Mortal Pearl within him throbbed warmly, seemingly responding to his fighting spirit.

"Night Shadow, flank from the left. Su Qinghan, hold his front. I'll look for openings."

"Understood!"

"Kill!"

---

All three struck simultaneously.

Night Shadow became a streak of shadow, circling toward the black-robed elder from the left.

Su Qinghan activated her ice true essence, forming dozens of ice shards before her, raining down upon the elder.

Lin Xuan activated the Reverse Mortal Pearl's power, a black current surging from within him, coiling around his sword.

His cultivation surged instantly.

Foundation Establishment mid stage — late stage — peak!

Three minor realms in succession!

The black-robed elder's expression finally changed.

"This is... the Reverse Mortal Pearl's power?!"

He retreated in shock, but it was already too late.

Lin Xuan's full-power sword strike had already reached him.

Sword light like a dragon, carrying the momentum of destruction.

"Clang!"

The elder barely blocked with his staff, but was sent flying several steps, blood spraying from his mouth.

"Despicable!" He roared. "Three brats think you can kill this old man?"

Black aura exploded from his body, a terrifying aura emanating forth.

Soul Extraction technique!

He was activating the Soul Extraction technique!

Lin Xuan's face changed — he immediately activated the method he just learned from the murals, channeling true essence to form a special barrier.

"Night Shadow, Su Qinghan, retreat!"

The elder inhaled, an invisible force rushing toward the three.

But this time, the force did not work as before.

Lin Xuan's true essence formed a barrier, isolating that absorption power.

"How?! How can you resist the Soul Extraction?!" The elder's eyes went wide.

"Because I know the weaknesses of your technique." Lin Xuan sneered. "I've long studied the Soul-Devouring Demon Ancestor's evil arts!"

He struck again, this time even more ferociously.

Night Shadow and Su Qinghan attacked simultaneously, their strikes forming a perfect coordination.

The elder, overwhelmed by attacks from all sides, gradually fell into disadvantage.

"No... impossible!" He howled madly. "I've cultivated the Soul Extraction for fifty years! How can I lose to three brats?!"

"Fifty years of evil arts cannot overcome a righteous heart!" Lin Xuan roared. "Die!"

His sword pierced through the elder's chest.

The elder's eyes bulged, disbelief filling his gaze.

"You... you..."

His words never finished, as he stopped breathing.

---

Lin Xuan slowly withdrew his sword, looking at the elder's corpse, but felt no joy in his heart.

This was merely the beginning.

The Xuantian Sect had far more powerful enemies.

But at least, they now possessed a weapon to counter them.

The existence of the "Demon Slaying Record" gave them hope.

"Let's go." He said. "Find a safe place to cultivate the Demon Slaying Record."

"Alright." Su Qinghan nodded.

Night Shadow also put away his weapons and approached.

The three left the ruin, disappearing into the depths of the Abyss of the Dead.

And behind them, another light glowed on that ancient altar.

From deep within the Soul-Devouring Demon Ancestor's seal, a low roar echoed.

Signs of awakening... were appearing.

But Lin Xuan didn't yet know — his opponents were far more than just the Xuantian Sect.

The cultivation world's greatest conspiracy was slowly being revealed.

And he would become the center of this storm.
