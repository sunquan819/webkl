## Budget Tier 1: 5000 Yuan Southeast Asia Islands

### Destination Recommendations: Thailand Phuket or Vietnam Nha Trang

Why recommend?
- Short flight time (4-5 hours)
- Cheap prices (accommodation, food, entertainment)
- Good beach quality
- Many Chinese services

### Itinerary Planning (5 Days)

**Day 1: Departure**
- Morning: Fly to Phuket or Nha Trang
- Afternoon: Check in hotel, rest
- Evening: Beach dinner, watch sunset

**Day 2: Beach Leisure**
- Morning: Beach swimming, sunbathing
- Afternoon: Water activities (snorkeling, jet ski)
- Evening: Seafood dinner, stroll night market

**Day 3: Island Day Trip**
- Morning: Boat to nearby islands
- Afternoon: Island exploration, snorkeling
- Evening: Return hotel, rest

**Day 4: Local Experience**
- Morning: Local markets, cultural sites
- Afternoon: Massage, SPA
- Evening: Bar street, nightlife

**Day 5: Return**
- Morning: Last beach time
- Afternoon: Airport return

### Budget Allocation (5000 Yuan)

Flight: 1500-2000 yuan (book early cheaper)
Accommodation: 1000 yuan (5 days, economy hotel)
Food: 800 yuan (local restaurants, seafood)
Activities: 700 yuan (water activities, day trip)
Transport: 500 yuan (taxi, boat tickets)
Others: 500 yuan (souvenirs, emergency)

### Money-Saving Tips

Flight savings:
- Book 1 month early
- Choose Tuesday Wednesday departure (lower price)
- Use comparison sites (Fliggy, Ctrip)

Accommodation savings:
- Choose hostels or economy hotels
- Not first row beach (expensive)
- Booking platform promotions

Food savings:
- Eat at local markets (cheap authentic)
- Avoid tourist area restaurants (expensive)
- Buy seafood yourself, restaurant process
