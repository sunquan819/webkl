## Chapter 8: The Truth of the Reverse Heaven One

The trial stone door slowly opened.

Lin Xuan and Su Qinghan walked into the stone chamber.

The chamber was not large. In the center stood a stone platform. On the platform rested a jade slip and a letter.

The jade slip emanated a faint glow, clearly a superior-grade magic artifact.

The letter had yellowed, clearly having been placed here for countless years.

Lin Xuan walked to the stone platform and picked up the letter.

On the envelope were four characters: For Those Who Come After.

Lin Xuan unfolded the letter. The handwriting within was bold and powerful:

"If you are reading this letter, it means you have passed the three trials and obtained the true inheritance of the Reverse Mortal Pearl."

"My name is Xiao Wuchen."

Su Qinghan's expression changed slightly upon hearing this name.

"Xiao Wuchen... the first generation sect master of Reverse Heaven Sect. Three hundred years ago, he was the most talented genius in the entire cultivation world. It's said he fell during a breakthrough, and no one knew the truth."

Lin Xuan continued reading:

"The history of the cultivation world is written by the victors. Three hundred years ago, I discovered the greatest secret of the cultivation world—all spiritual roots are artificially created."

"Spiritual roots are nothing but control mechanisms left by ancient great ones. People without spiritual roots are the true geniuses. They possess infinite potential, unbound by any rules."

"The Reverse Mortal Pearl is the only treasure from ancient times that can awaken those without spiritual roots. But the cultivation world's high authorities view it as forbidden, because it threatens their rule."

"I was hunted by the entire cultivation world and ultimately fell here. But I do not regret it."

"If you can read this letter, it means you, like me, are one chosen by fate. The Reverse Mortal Pearl will help you, but it also has a cost. Each time you use it, you move one step further from your true self."

"I chose to seal everything here, waiting for one who would come after."

"May you complete the unfinished cause I could not."

No signature appeared at the letter's end.

Lin Xuan finished reading and fell into silence for a long while.

Su Qinghan said, "The truth from three hundred years ago... is what Xiao Wuchen said true?"

Lin Xuan said, "Whether it's true doesn't matter. What matters is, I now have power."

He picked up the jade slip and pressed it to his forehead.

A flood of information poured into his mind.

The true ability of the Reverse Mortal Pearl was not merely changing meridians.

It could absorb others' spiritual roots and convert them into its own power.

This was forbidden among the forbidden.

Lin Xuan's eyes deepened.

He finally understood why the Reverse Mortal Pearl had chosen him.

Because he should not have existed in this world.

Hidden within his body lay a secret that had been erased.

---

Upon returning to Xuantian Sect, Lin Xuan began secretly investigating his own origins.

He discovered that thirty years ago, a disciple named Lin Yuanshan was expelled from the sect. The charge was "colluding with the demonic path."

But in truth, Lin Yuanshan had discovered a sect secret—the high authorities of Xuantian Sect had been secretly collecting the vital essence of those without spiritual roots for some forbidden ritual.

Lin Yuanshan was executed, but his wife gave birth to a child while fleeing.

That child was Lin Xuan.

His spiritual root was artificially implanted.

He should not have possessed a spiritual root, yet was forced to become a "person with spiritual roots."

All of this was arranged by the sect.

Lin Xuan's fingers trembled slightly.

His fate had been designed by others from the very beginning.

Su Qinghan stood beside him, silent.

She knew that what Lin Xuan needed now was not comfort, but power.

"What should we do?" she asked.

Lin Xuan's gaze was ice-cold:

"Find those behind it all."

"Then make them pay the price."
