## Chapter 4: Encounter in Nether Abyss

Lin Xuan's right arm meridians had been severed for three days.

He didn't seek treatment, didn't complain. He simply practiced swordplay with his left hand.

Pain kept him awake. Being awake helped him remember the cost.

On the fourth morning, a messenger disciple arrived at his stone hut.

"Lin Xuan, the Elder summons you."

Lin Xuan rose, securing his right arm to his chest with cloth strips, and followed the messenger disciple toward the Outer Sect Hall.

---

In the hall, the Outer Sect Elder sat on a stone chair, his expression cold.

"Lin Xuan, your right arm meridians are severed. You can no longer cultivate. But the sect won't waste a disciple who can still fight."

"I'm sending you to Nether Abyss."

Lin Xuan frowned.

Nether Abyss—the forbidden grounds of Mystic Sky Sect. Legend said it was an ancient battlefield ruin, filled with chaotic spiritual energy and rampaging beasts. Disciples who entered faced near-certain death.

"Why me?"

"Because you have no spiritual root." The Elder said. "The spiritual energy in Nether Abyss is chaotic. Disciples with spiritual roots would suffer backlash. You have none, so you won't be affected."

"What if I refuse?"

The Elder sneered: "Your right arm meridians are severed, your spirit stones confiscated. If you refuse the mission, you'll be expelled from the sect immediately."

Lin Xuan fell silent.

"Fine. I'll go."

The Elder nodded and tossed him a token.

"This is the Nether Abyss passage token. Depart at midnight today. No delays."

Lin Xuan caught the token and turned to leave the hall.

---

Midnight, behind the mountains of Mystic Sky Sect.

Lin Xuan arrived at the entrance to Nether Abyss. Below the cliff, black mist swirled, the depths invisible.

But he wasn't alone.

At the cliff's edge stood a white-robed woman.

She appeared about seventeen or eighteen, her face cold, her eyes like ice. She wore a white long dress, an ice-blue sword hanging at her waist.

Lin Xuan stopped.

"Who are you?"

The woman turned to look at him, her eyes indifferent.

"Su Qinghan. Outer Sect disciple."

"You're also going to Nether Abyss?"

Su Qinghan nodded: "The Elder sent me. Said there are ancient ice-attribute techniques in Nether Abyss, suitable for my ice spiritual root."

Lin Xuan frowned.

"You came alone?"

Su Qinghan looked at him.

"Your right arm meridians are severed, yet you also came alone. We're the same."

Lin Xuan said nothing.

Su Qinghan continued: "The Elder said we should cooperate. You scout, I fight. Whatever we find, we each take what we need."

"Take what we need?"

"I want the ancient ice-attribute technique. What you want, I don't know." Su Qinghan said. "But I know Nether Abyss is dangerous. If we cooperate, our chances of survival are greater."

Lin Xuan looked at her.

"Aren't you afraid I'll slow you down?"

Su Qinghan shook her head.

"You've hunted a second-tier spirit beast, the Black Bear. Even with your right arm ruined, you survived. That proves you have skill."

She turned to look down at the abyss.

"Besides, I don't need anyone's protection. I need a companion."

Something stirred in Lin Xuan's heart.

This woman relied on no one. She was independent, proud, yet rational.

"Fine. Cooperation."

Su Qinghan nodded.

"Let's go."

---

The two climbed down ropes to the bottom of Nether Abyss.

At the bottom, rocks were damp, black mist permeated the air.

After landing, Su Qinghan drew her ice-blue longsword. The blade emanated cold energy, dispersing the surrounding black mist.

"Stay close to me." She said. "Ice techniques can disperse the black mist, but not for long."

Lin Xuan nodded, gripping his iron sword in his left hand. The Reverse Mortal Pearl vibrated in his palm.

They proceeded carefully.

After walking about ten minutes, Su Qinghan suddenly stopped.

"Spirit beasts ahead."

Lin Xuan held his breath.

From the black mist ahead, three gray beasts appeared.

First-tier spirit beasts: Gray Wolves.

Su Qinghan didn't hesitate. Her ice-blue longsword slashed out.

Cold energy erupted. The three Gray Wolves were instantly frozen.

Su Qinghan stepped forward, her sword tip piercing the wolves' throats—each strike fatal.

Clean and precise movements, no wasted flourish.

Lin Xuan watched her, silently impressed.

This woman had extensive combat experience.

"Moving on." Su Qinghan sheathed her sword and continued forward.

---

After another ten minutes, a cave appeared ahead.

The cave entrance was sealed by runes, but they were already damaged.

"This is it." Su Qinghan said. "An ancient ruin."

They entered the cave.

The cave was spacious inside. Murals covered the walls.

The murals depicted a great battle.

People in black robes fought against people in white robes. The black-robed figures held strange pearls that glowed with dark red light.

The white-robed figures used spiritual energy techniques, but the black-robed figures' pearls could reverse spiritual energy, reflecting the techniques back.

The battle was brutal, both sides suffering heavy casualties.

In the final mural, the black-robed figures buried the pearls underground, then all committed suicide.

Lin Xuan stared at the murals, deeply shaken.

The Reverse Mortal Pearl vibrated even more intensely.

Dark red light shone through the cracks on the pearl's surface—exactly like the pearls in the murals.

"So you're not an ordinary pearl." Lin Xuan whispered.

Su Qinghan looked at the murals, her expression complex.

"This is the legend of the ancient Reverse Mortals." She said. "My family has studied ancient history for generations. Reverse Mortals—those who don't cultivate spiritual energy, don't worship the Heavenly Dao, don't submit to fate. They reverse heaven and earth with mortal bodies."

Lin Xuan turned to look at her.

"Your family?"

Su Qinghan fell silent.

"The Su family, descendants of ancient Reverse Mortals. Three hundred years ago, the Su family was massacred."

Her voice was calm, but Lin Xuan heard the hatred beneath.

"Why?"

"Because the Su family possessed the power of reversal. The sects feared them and united to destroy us." Su Qinghan said. "Before my father died, he passed the ice techniques to me. He told me to survive, to take revenge."

Lin Xuan looked at her.

"So you came to Nether Abyss not just for techniques."

Su Qinghan nodded.

"I need to find the Reverse Mortal Pearl. The Su family records say the pearl is the core of reversal power. With it, I can take revenge."

She turned to look at Lin Xuan.

"But you've already obtained it."

Lin Xuan gripped the Reverse Mortal Pearl tightly.

"The pearl chose me, not the Su family."

Su Qinghan looked at him, her expression complex.

"I know. I won't take it."

She put away her longsword.

"I, Su Qinghan, always repay debts and avenge wrongs. You don't owe me, I don't owe you. But if we cooperate, I'll keep my promise."

Something stirred in Lin Xuan's heart.

This woman relied on no one. She had revenge to take, debts to repay. She was independent, but principled.

"Fine. Cooperation."

Su Qinghan nodded.

"Find what we need, each takes their share."

---

Deep in the cave, there was a stone platform.

On the platform lay two jade slips.

One was ice-blue, the other dark red.

Su Qinghan approached the ice-blue jade slip. Lin Xuan approached the dark red one.

They both picked up the jade slips simultaneously.

In the ice-blue jade slip was an ancient ice technique: the "Frost Art."

In the dark red jade slip was the method to use the power of reversal.

"The power of reversal is not a treasure of spiritual energy, but a technique of reversal. It can reverse injuries, reverse spiritual energy, reverse fate. But each reversal requires a price. The greater the price, the stronger the power."

Lin Xuan gripped the jade slip tightly.

Su Qinghan also gripped the ice-blue jade slip.

"I found it." She said. "Thank you for your cooperation."

Lin Xuan nodded.

"Each takes what they need."

---

But footsteps came from outside the cave.

They both stopped.

Three disciples in Mystic Sky Sect robes entered the cave.

But their expressions were strange.

Hollow eyes, stiff movements—as if controlled by something.

"Puppet cultivators." Lin Xuan's heart trembled.

Su Qinghan also recognized it.

"The sect is using disciples for puppet experiments." She whispered. "I've heard this rumor. I didn't think it was true."

The three puppet cultivators saw them and immediately lunged.

Su Qinghan didn't hesitate. Her ice-blue longsword slashed out.

Cold energy erupted. The first puppet cultivator was frozen.

But puppet cultivators felt no pain. They broke free from the ice and continued attacking.

"They have no sense of pain." Su Qinghan said. "We must find the runes controlling them."

Lin Xuan observed the puppet cultivators' movements.

"The neck." He said. "There are control runes on their necks."

Su Qinghan nodded.

"I'll freeze them, you destroy the runes."

"Right."

Su Qinghan swung her sword. Cold energy erupted, freezing all three puppet cultivators.

Lin Xuan stepped forward, his iron sword piercing the first puppet cultivator's neck rune.

The rune shattered. The puppet cultivator fell, consciousness returning.

"Th-thank you..." the puppet cultivator said weakly, then fainted.

The second and third were freed the same way.

Su Qinghan sheathed her sword, looking at the three fallen disciples.

"The sect is doing such things." She said. "Using disciples for puppet experiments."

Lin Xuan was silent.

"We must tell more people."

Su Qinghan shook her head.

"Tell who? The Elders? The high-ranking members? They're the ones behind this."

Lin Xuan frowned.

"Then what do we do?"

Su Qinghan looked at him.

"Get stronger. Strong enough to stand against the sect."

She turned, preparing to leave the cave.

"Lin Xuan."

"Yes?"

"Today you helped me obtain the Frost Art. I, Su Qinghan, always repay kindness. If you're in danger in the future, I'll help you."

Lin Xuan looked at her.

"Why? We've only known each other a day."

Su Qinghan looked back, her eyes cold and clear.

"Because you didn't abandon me in Nether Abyss. Because the way you look at me isn't the way you look at the weak. Because you said 'cooperate,' not 'I'll protect you.'"

She turned and walked out of the cave.

"I, Su Qinghan, depend on no one. But I acknowledge companions."

Lin Xuan gripped the Reverse Mortal Pearl and followed.

---

The two returned to the cliff, climbing the ropes to the top.

Dawn had already broken.

Su Qinghan turned to look at Lin Xuan.

"Don't tell anyone about today."

Lin Xuan nodded.

"Same to you."

Su Qinghan nodded.

"We'll need to return to Nether Abyss in the future."

"Mm."

Su Qinghan turned and walked away.

After a few steps, she stopped.

"Lin Xuan."

"Yes?"

"Your right arm meridians are severed. I know a physician. I'll take you next time."

"Why help me?"

Su Qinghan didn't look back.

"I said it before. I always repay kindness."

She disappeared into the forest.

---

Lin Xuan stood alone at the cliff's edge, holding the Reverse Mortal Pearl.

The pearl vibrated, dark red light flickering.

The power of reversal.

The legacy of ancient Reverse Mortals.

The sect's conspiracy.

Puppet cultivator experiments.

Su Qinghan's secret.

Revenge. Gratitude.

All of this was just the beginning.

Lin Xuan turned and walked toward the Outer Sect stone hut.

His right arm meridians were severed, but the Reverse Mortal Pearl's power was growing.

He knew the next battle would be even more brutal.

But he wouldn't cry, wouldn't kneel, wouldn't beg.
