## Chapter 3: Outer Gate Trial

The outer gate of Xuantian Sect lies in a valley beneath the Cliffpeak.

Over three hundred outer gate disciples live here. Their quarters are crude stone huts, and their daily tasks consist of cultivation, chores, and fighting for resources.

Lin Xuan was assigned to the most remote stone hut.

The hut was small, containing only a stone bed and a wooden table. Wind leaked through the roof, and the walls were damp.

On the first night, Lin Xuan sat on the stone bed, holding the Reverse Mortal Pearl.

The pearl was dull, without any luster.

But Lin Xuan could feel a faint vibration within it. That vibration synchronized with his heartbeat.

"What are you?" Lin Xuan asked quietly.

The Reverse Mortal Pearl did not respond.

---

Early the next morning, a messenger disciple from the outer gate elder arrived at the hut.

"All newly admitted disciples will participate in the outer gate trial today," the messenger said coldly. "Location: Beast Hunting Valley."

Lin Xuan rose and followed the messenger toward Beast Hunting Valley.

Beast Hunting Valley was located in the back mountains of Xuantian Sect, a valley sealed with runes. Inside lived various low-tier demonic beasts for disciples to practice against.

But the rules of the outer gate trial were cruel.

Only the top ten disciples in each trial would receive spirit stone rewards. The rest would not only receive nothing but have their own spirit stones deducted.

If a disciple's spirit stones were depleted, they would be expelled from the sect.

---

At the entrance of Beast Hunting Valley, over fifty new disciples had gathered.

Lin Xuan stood among the crowd, observing his surroundings.

Most of these disciples were fifteen or sixteen years old, with varying spiritual root qualities. Some had excellent roots and were full of confidence. Others had poor roots and looked nervous.

A disciple in blue robes walked up to Lin Xuan.

"So you're the one with no spiritual root?" the blue-robed disciple sneered. "I heard you forced your way into the sect, and the elders are not pleased."

Lin Xuan looked at him.

The blue-robed disciple continued, "The beasts in the valley don't care whether you have a spiritual root or not. They only care about eating people."

Lin Xuan said nothing.

The blue-robed disciple turned away and approached another disciple.

Lin Xuan noticed that disciple had decent spiritual root quality. The blue-robed disciple clearly wanted to ally with useful companions, while someone like Lin Xuan with no spiritual root was trash in his eyes.

---

The trial began.

The outer gate elder stood at the entrance of Beast Hunting Valley and activated the runes.

"The trial lasts one day. The more beasts you hunt, the higher your rank. The top ten receive spirit stone rewards, the bottom ten have spirit stones deducted. Those whose stones are depleted will be expelled from the sect."

After speaking, the elder opened the rune seal.

Over fifty disciples flooded into Beast Hunting Valley.

---

Inside Beast Hunting Valley, the trees were dense and the light dim.

Lin Xuan advanced cautiously, holding an iron sword. It was a weapon given by the sect upon entry, of very poor quality.

After walking for ten minutes, Lin Xuan heard movement to his left.

He stopped and held his breath.

From the bushes on the left, a gray demonic beast emerged.

It was a first-tier demonic beast: a gray wolf.

The gray wolf was twice the size of an ordinary wolf, with sharp teeth and great speed.

Lin Xuan gripped his iron sword tightly.

The gray wolf stared at Lin Xuan and slowly approached.

At five meters, the gray wolf suddenly lunged at Lin Xuan.

Lin Xuan sidestepped and thrust his iron sword toward the wolf's belly.

But the sword was of such poor quality that it only penetrated half an inch before being caught by the wolf's fur.

The gray wolf turned and swiped its claws at Lin Xuan.

Lin Xuan retreated, but not fast enough. The wolf's claws tore through his left arm, and blood flowed.

The pain brought Lin Xuan back to clarity.

He gripped the Reverse Mortal Pearl and whispered, "Help me."

The Reverse Mortal Pearl showed no reaction.

The gray wolf lunged again.

This time Lin Xuan did not dodge. He faced the wolf head-on, thrusting his iron sword toward its throat.

The wolf's teeth bit into Lin Xuan's shoulder as the sword pierced its throat.

Blood sprayed.

The gray wolf fell, twitched a few times, and stopped breathing.

Lin Xuan's shoulder was bitten open, blood flowing continuously. He sat on the ground, breathing heavily.

The Reverse Mortal Pearl vibrated once.

Lin Xuan felt a force flowing from the pearl into his body. It was cold, like ice water.

His wound began to heal. Slowly, but it was healing.

---

Lin Xuan dragged the gray wolf's corpse out of Beast Hunting Valley.

The outer gate elder stood at the entrance, recording the number of beasts each disciple had hunted.

"One first-tier gray wolf," the elder said indifferently. "Rank: fortieth."

Lin Xuan said nothing.

The elder continued, "The bottom ten in the outer gate trial have their spirit stones deducted. You have no spirit stones and will be expelled from the sect."

Lin Xuan looked up at the elder.

"I hunted a beast. Why am I in the bottom ten?"

The elder sneered, "Other disciples hunted more beasts. You rank fortieth, which places you in the bottom ten."

Lin Xuan fell silent.

---

Night fell, and Lin Xuan returned to his stone hut.

His shoulder and left arm were still in pain. The healing power of the Reverse Mortal Pearl was weak and had only closed half the wounds.

Lin Xuan sat on the stone bed, thinking about his situation tomorrow.

Being expelled from the sect meant he could not continue cultivation.

But he did not want to leave.

Lin Xuan gripped the Reverse Mortal Pearl and whispered, "What will you do if I'm expelled?"

The Reverse Mortal Pearl vibrated once.

A fine crack appeared on the pearl's surface. From within the crack, a dark red light seeped out.

The light was cold, carrying a cruel aura.

Lin Xuan felt the power within the Reverse Mortal Pearl beginning to awaken.

That power was not spiritual energy, but something else.

Something colder, more ruthless, more suited for survival.

---

Early the next morning, a messenger disciple arrived at the hut.

"Lin Xuan, the elder summons you."

Lin Xuan rose and followed the messenger to the outer gate hall.

In the hall, the outer gate elder sat on a stone chair, his expression cold.

"Lin Xuan, your spirit stones are depleted, and you will be expelled from the sect," the elder said. "Leave today, no delays."

Lin Xuan stood before the elder.

"I do not want to leave."

The elder frowned, "You have no choice. These are the sect's rules."

Lin Xuan gripped the Reverse Mortal Pearl.

"Rules can be changed."

The elder sneered, "What right does someone with no spiritual root have to change the rules?"

Lin Xuan looked at the elder.

"The right to survive."

The elder fell silent.

Lin Xuan continued, "In the Beast Hunting Valley trial, I hunted a gray wolf. Though my rank was low, I proved I can fight. If the sect expels me, it wastes a disciple who can fight."

The elder shook his head, "Fighting requires spiritual energy. Without a spiritual root, you can never truly cultivate."

Lin Xuan took out the Reverse Mortal Pearl.

"I have other methods."

The elder looked at the pearl and frowned, "What is this?"

Lin Xuan said, "I don't know. But it lets me fight."

The elder fell silent.

---

Outside the hall, the murmurs of disciples could be heard.

"That person with no spiritual root actually refuses to leave."

"What right does he have to stay?"

"The elder should just drive him out directly."

Lin Xuan listened to these discussions, his expression calm.

After a moment of silence, the elder finally spoke.

"Lin Xuan, I can let you stay. But there is a condition."

Lin Xuan asked, "What condition?"

The elder said, "In three days, a second-tier demonic beast will appear in Beast Hunting Valley. Second-tier beasts are three times stronger than first-tier ones. If you can hunt one, I will allow you to remain in the outer gate. Otherwise, immediate expulsion."

Lin Xuan nodded, "I accept."

The elder waved his hand, "Leave. Prepare for the trial in three days."

---

Lin Xuan walked out of the hall and returned to his stone hut.

He sat on the stone bed, holding the Reverse Mortal Pearl.

"Hunt a second-tier beast in three days," Lin Xuan said quietly. "Are you ready?"

The Reverse Mortal Pearl vibrated once.

The crack on the pearl's surface widened, and the dark red light grew brighter.

Lin Xuan felt the power within the pearl.

It was cold, carrying a bloody aura.

As if the Reverse Mortal Pearl was telling him:

"I can help you hunt a second-tier beast. But the cost is that the meridians in your right arm will break."

Lin Xuan fell silent.

He looked at his right arm.

To hunt a second-tier beast meant paying the price of broken meridians in his right arm.

This was the Reverse Mortal Pearl's first true test.

---

Lin Xuan gripped the Reverse Mortal Pearl.

"I accept the cost."

The dark red light of the Reverse Mortal Pearl grew brighter.

More cracks appeared on the pearl's surface, as if responding to Lin Xuan's choice.

---

Three days later, Beast Hunting Valley.

Second-tier demonic beast: black bear, massive in size, with hard fur and astonishing strength.

Lin Xuan stood alone in the depths of Beast Hunting Valley, watching the black bear approach.

The Reverse Mortal Pearl vibrated in his hand, its dark red light flashing.

Lin Xuan gripped his iron sword, ready for battle.

The black bear lunged.

Lin Xuan faced it head-on, the power of the Reverse Mortal Pearl flooding into his right arm.

The iron sword thrust toward the bear's throat as the pearl's power erupted.

The black bear fell.

But Lin Xuan's right arm meridians shattered, blood spraying.

He fell to his knees, his right arm unable to move.

The pain nearly made him lose consciousness.

The Reverse Mortal Pearl vibrated, its dark red light consuming the blood.

More cracks appeared on the pearl's surface, as if absorbing the cost Lin Xuan had paid.

---

At the entrance of Beast Hunting Valley, the elder recorded the hunt count.

"One second-tier black bear," the elder said in silence. "Lin Xuan, you may remain in the outer gate."

Lin Xuan dragged the black bear's corpse, his right arm hanging limp at his side, unable to move.

His expression was cold, showing no sign of pain.

The surrounding disciples watched him, some in awe, others in mockery.

"He really hunted a second-tier beast."

"But his right arm is ruined."

"A person with no spiritual root, fighting this way, truly ruthless."

Lin Xuan paid no attention to these discussions.

He returned to his stone hut, sat on the stone bed, and held the Reverse Mortal Pearl.

The pearl's surface was covered in cracks, its dark red light flickering.

Lin Xuan felt the power within the pearl.

It was stronger now, and colder.

As if the Reverse Mortal Pearl was telling him:

"Cost paid. Power gained. The next battle will be stronger."

Lin Xuan looked at the broken meridians in his right arm.

He knew this was the Reverse Mortal Pearl's first true cost.

There would be more to come.

---

Late at night, Lin Xuan lay on the stone bed, his right arm in unbearable pain.

But he did not cry, did not shout, did not beg for mercy.

He simply held the Reverse Mortal Pearl, enduring the pain in silence.

The Reverse Mortal Pearl vibrated, its dark red light flickering.

As if responding to Lin Xuan's silence:

"No crying, no kneeling, no begging. This is good. Only such a person can reverse fate."

Lin Xuan closed his eyes and fell asleep.

Tomorrow, he would continue to survive.

---

**Outer Gate Trial concluded.**

Lin Xuan remained in the outer gate of Xuantian Sect, his right arm meridians broken, but the power of the Reverse Mortal Pearl was stronger.

He began surviving in blood.

Cost paid, power gained.

The path of reversing fate had officially begun.
