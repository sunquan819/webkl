## Chapter 10: Declaration of War

In the elder council chamber, the atmosphere dropped to freezing point.

After Lin Xuan finished speaking, the entire hall fell into deathly silence.

Everyone stared in shock at this young man — he actually dared to expose the sect's secrets at the elder council, in front of the sect master.

Xuanmingzi's face became extremely ugly.

"Lin Xuan, do you know what you're saying?" His voice was ice-cold. "You're accusing the entire sect's high authorities? Do you have evidence?"

"I do." Lin Xuan took out a stack of jade slips from his chest. "This is all the evidence I collected. Thirty years ago, twenty-three people without spiritual roots were secretly executed. The sect's Soul Extraction Technique requires their essence to practice."

He threw the jade slips into the air. They floated in the center of the hall, projecting large amounts of text and images.

They were the sect's secret archives.

"This is..." The law enforcement elder's face changed dramatically. "How did you obtain these?"

"Xiao Wuchen left them to me." Lin Xuan said. "His old friend Lin Yuanshan discovered this secret, so you executed him. But you didn't expect that Lin Yuanshan still had a son alive."

Xuanmingzi's pupils contracted violently.

"You're Lin Yuanshan's son?"

"Yes." Lin Xuan stared directly at the sect master. "Thirty years ago, you killed my father. Thirty years later, I've come to seek justice from you."

The hall erupted in an uproar.

Several elders began whispering to each other. Clearly, Lin Yuanshan's case had always been controversial within the sect.

"Enough!" Xuanmingzi slammed his chair and stood up. "Lin Xuan, you fabricated evidence and defamed the sect. The crime is compounded. Seize him!"

A dozen law enforcement disciples rushed into the hall, surrounding Lin Xuan completely.

Su Qinghan and Night Shadow simultaneously stepped in front of Lin Xuan.

"Sect master." Su Qinghan's voice was cold and clear. "What Lin Xuan said might be true."

"What did you say?" Xuanmingzi raged.

"I've investigated Lin Yuanshan's case." Su Qinghan said. "The evidence back then was not sufficient, many places were questionable. If you truly are innocent, why don't you dare let the sect re-examine this case?"

Xuanmingzi's face turned completely dark.

"Treason! All of you are treasonous!" He roared. "Seize Su Qinghan and Night Shadow as well!"

The law enforcement disciples hesitated but still rushed forward.

Lin Xuan knew the moment had finally come.

He took a deep breath, and the Reverse Mortal Pearl within him began to circulate wildly.

A powerful force exploded from within him.

"Who dares touch him!"

A golden glow flashed in Lin Xuan's eyes.

His aura instantly skyrocketed — from Foundation Building stage directly broke through to Core Formation, then continued rising — Core Formation mid-stage, Core Formation late-stage...

Finally stopping at Nascent Soul early stage.

The entire hall was shrouded in this pressure.

The law enforcement disciples all retreated, faces pale.

"Nascent Soul stage..." An elder exclaimed. "He broke through to Nascent Soul stage!"

Xuanmingzi's pupils contracted violently.

Nascent Soul stage — that was the realm only the sect master had. How long had Lin Xuan been cultivating? How could he reach this realm?

"It's the Reverse Mortal Pearl's power." Night Shadow murmured. "He traded lifespan for a burst of power..."

Su Qinghan looked at Lin Xuan, eyes slightly reddened.

She knew Lin Xuan was burning his own life.

"Go!" Lin Xuan shouted, grabbed Su Qinghan and Night Shadow, and transformed into a streak of light rushing out of the hall.

"Stop them!" Xuanmingzi roared.

But Lin Xuan at Nascent Soul stage was unbelievably fast.

In the blink of an eye, he had broken out of the elder hall and vanished into the night.

---

Half an hour later, Lin Xuan appeared on a mountain peak a hundred li from the sect.

His face was pale as paper, a trace of blood at the corner of his mouth.

The power burst just now had placed enormous strain on his body.

"Lin Xuan!" Su Qinghan supported him, face full of worry. "Your body..."

"I'm fine." Lin Xuan forced a smile. "Just overdrawn a bit."

"A bit?" Night Shadow frowned. "You just consumed at least ten years of lifespan."

Lin Xuan said nothing.

Ten years of lifespan exchanged for power — worth it.

"What do we do now?" Su Qinghan asked.

Lin Xuan raised his head, gazing toward the sect's direction, eyes flickering with cold light.

"We need allies." He said. "The sect won't let us go. We need to find other forces harmed by the Soul Extraction Technique and unite against Xuantian Sect."

"You're planning to expose the sect's crimes to the entire cultivation world?"

"Yes." Lin Xuan said. "But before that, we need to grow stronger."

He took out a map from his chest.

"According to what Xiao Wuchen left behind, there are more secrets deep within the Nether Abyss. If we can find those, perhaps we can find a way to deal with the Soul Extraction Technique."

Su Qinghan looked at the map, silent for a moment.

"I'm going with you." She said.

"I'm coming too." Night Shadow said. "The Shadow Hall has long been dissatisfied with Xuanmingzi. This matter gives us exactly an opportunity."

Lin Xuan looked at the two of them, warmth rising in his heart.

In this world, he was no longer alone.

"Good." He said. "Together."

In the distance, toward Xuantian Sect, flames lit up the sky.

The sect had already issued a hunt order.

Lin Xuan, Su Qinghan, and Night Shadow embarked on the path of fleeing and revenge.

And behind them, a enormous conspiracy was slowly being revealed.

The true secrets of the Reverse Mortal Pearl, the origin of the Soul Extraction Technique, and the greatest secret of the entire cultivation world — all awaited them.
