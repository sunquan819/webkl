## Chapter 1: Jumping Off the Cliff

Beneath the Xuantian Sect, over a thousand youths waited to test spiritual roots.

Spiritual roots determined fate. Those with spiritual roots cultivated for immortality. Those without died in a hundred years.

This was the cultivation world's law.

Lin Xuan stood at the back of the line. Sixteen years old, walked from Lin Family Village, begged along the way, nearly starved.

He knew his situation. Without spiritual roots, he could only accept fate. Those who accepted fate died in a hundred years. Those who didn't—the cultivation world didn't allow them to exist.

Ahead in the line, someone detected spiritual roots, knelt to thank. Someone didn't, cried and begged.

The white-robed disciple looked at them coldly: "No spiritual roots. Eliminated."

No mercy, no sympathy. The cultivation world was like this.

Finally Lin Xuan's turn.

The white-robed disciple took out the testing disk. Lin Xuan placed his hand on it.

No reaction.

The white-robed disciple said: "No spiritual roots. Eliminated."

Lin Xuan withdrew his hand, turned to leave.

He didn't cry, didn't kneel, didn't beg. He just looked at the white-robed disciple once, eyes ice-cold.

The white-robed disciple frowned: "You still won't leave?"

Lin Xuan didn't answer.

He walked ten steps, stopped.

Looked up at Xuantian Sect's summit. There was the world of cultivators. Beneath his feet was dirt, the world of mortals.

Lin Xuan took out a black stone from his chest. Picked it from a dead person, didn't know what use it had.

He gripped the stone.

"No spiritual roots, cannot cultivate. This is the cultivation world's law." Lin Xuan whispered. "If I accept this law, I am mortal. Mortals die in a hundred years."

He laughed.

A cold laugh.

"But I don't accept."

He turned, walked toward the rear mountain's Juebi Peak.

Juebi Peak was forbidden territory. Legend said many died there.

Lin Xuan walked to Juebi Peak's edge. Below was an abyss, couldn't see the bottom.

"Those without spiritual roots can only accept fate. Accepting fate is following. Those who follow laws die." Lin Xuan gripped the black stone tighter. "I won't follow. I will reverse."

He looked at the abyss.

"Those who reverse laws might die faster. But at least I tried."

He jumped down.

Wind whistled. Lin Xuan plummeted.

Just about to crash into the ground, the black stone in his hand vibrated.

An ice-cold force surged out, rushed into Lin Xuan's body.

Meridians severe pain.

That force forcibly rushed through blocked meridians.

Lin Xuan grit his teeth, didn't scream.

He knew this was opportunity. Mortals without spiritual roots had blocked meridians. But this force was forcibly opening them.

The pain lasted a long time.

Lin Xuan felt he was about to die. But he didn't let go.

Finally, he landed on a platform.

The platform's surroundings were carved full of runes, emitting blue light.

At the center was a stone tablet. On the tablet was written:

"This stone is named Reverse Mortal Pearl. Those who obtain this pearl can reverse the cultivation world's laws, forcibly cultivating with a mortal body. Cost: lifetime tribulations, nine deaths for one life. If successful, can transcend laws, achieve true immortal."

Lin Xuan looked at the stone tablet.

The black stone's shell fell off, revealing a gray pearl. On the pearl was carved a human figure, standing on a shattered road, the road extended toward the sky.

Lin Xuan gripped the pearl, felt the surging force.

In his meridians, a weak stream of qi began to flow.

That was spiritual qi.

Lin Xuan closed his eyes to feel.

Very weak, but it definitely existed.

He could finally cultivate.

The cost was "lifetime tribulations, nine deaths for one life."

Lin Xuan was silent for a long time.

Then he laughed.

A cold laugh.

"I, Lin Xuan, have nothing. Even if nine deaths for one life, it's better than dying silently in a hundred years."

He gripped the Reverse Mortal Pearl tighter.

"The cultivation world's laws say those without spiritual roots can only accept fate. I don't accept. I will reverse this law."
