## Chapter 5: The Physician's Secret

Three days after the Nether Abyss mission, Lin Xuan's right arm meridians remained severed and painful.

The Reverse Mortal Pearl's power had stopped the bleeding, but could not fully repair the meridians. If this continued, his right arm might be permanently crippled.

Su Qinghan arrived at the stone house door.

"Come with me," she said. "I'll take you to find a physician."

Lin Xuan looked up at her.

"A physician? Does Xuantian Sect have a physician?"

Su Qinghan nodded.

"An outer sect physician, but she doesn't reside within the sect. She's in the medicine garden deep in the valley."

Lin Xuan rose and followed Su Qinghan out of the stone house.

---

The medicine garden was located deep in Xuantian Sect's rear mountain, sealed by runes that prevented outsiders from entering.

Su Qinghan led Lin Xuan through the rune seal into the medicine garden.

The medicine garden was quiet, planted with various herbs. The air was filled with the scent of medicine.

At the center of the garden stood a wooden house.

The door opened, and a woman in green walked out.

The woman appeared about thirty, with a gentle face and deep eyes. She wore a green long dress, with a medicine pouch hanging at her waist.

"Qinghan, you've come," the woman said.

Su Qinghan nodded.

"Physician, I've brought a friend. His right arm meridians are severed, he needs treatment."

The woman looked at Lin Xuan, her expression complex.

"Severed meridians?" She approached and examined Lin Xuan's right arm.

Her fingers lightly touched Lin Xuan's arm, and he felt a gentle power flow in.

"The meridians are completely severed, they cannot repair naturally," the woman said. "A special method is needed."

"What method?" Lin Xuan asked.

The woman fell silent.

"The reversal method."

Lin Xuan gripped the Reverse Mortal Pearl.

"Reversal?"

The woman nodded.

"Reversing the meridian severance, reconnecting the broken points. But the cost is great."

"What cost?"

The woman looked at Su Qinghan.

"Qinghan, you know what the cost is."

Su Qinghan remained silent.

"I know."

Lin Xuan looked at both of them.

"What cost? Why do you both know?"

The woman sighed.

"Because I am of the Su family."

---

Lin Xuan's heart trembled.

"Su family?"

The woman nodded.

"Before the Su family massacre, I escaped the Su family and hid here. I used my medical skills in exchange for the sect's protection."

She looked at Lin Xuan.

"Qinghan is a Su family descendant. She came to me because she knows I can treat severed meridians."

Lin Xuan turned to look at Su Qinghan.

"You knew the physician was Su family?"

Su Qinghan nodded.

"I knew. But I didn't tell you."

"Why?"

Su Qinghan remained silent.

"Because the physician has old grudges with my family."

---

The woman sighed.

"Qinghan's grandfather was my brother. When the massacre happened, he didn't notify me to escape. I survived because I left the Su family early."

She looked at Su Qinghan.

"Qinghan hates me because she thinks I betrayed the family."

Su Qinghan said coldly.

"You didn't betray the family. You just escaped early. But your escape left the family unprepared. When the massacre happened, the family suffered even greater losses."

The woman remained silent.

"I know my escape put the family in greater danger. But I cannot change the past."

She looked at Lin Xuan.

"I can treat your severed meridians. But there is one condition."

Lin Xuan asked.

"What condition?"

The woman said.

"Help me complete a mission."

"What mission?"

The woman was silent for a moment.

"Kill someone."

---

Lin Xuan frowned.

"Kill someone?"

The woman nodded.

"A sect elder, Elder Zhang. He was one of the masterminds behind the Su family massacre."

Lin Xuan's heart trembled.

"Elder Zhang? The outer sect elder?"

The woman nodded.

"Outer sect Elder Zhang, who participated in the Su family massacre three hundred years ago. He still lives today, holding power in the sect."

She looked at Lin Xuan.

"You help me kill Elder Zhang, and I'll treat your severed meridians."

Lin Xuan remained silent.

Su Qinghan looked at him.

"Lin Xuan, this is revenge."

Lin Xuan looked at Su Qinghan.

"Revenge?"

Su Qinghan nodded.

"Elder Zhang participated in the Su family massacre. Killing him is revenge."

She looked at the woman.

"Physician, why don't you kill Elder Zhang yourself?"

The woman sighed.

"Though my medical skills are strong, my combat ability is weak. I cannot fight an elder."

She looked at Lin Xuan.

"You've hunted a second-level demonic beast, the Black Bear. You have combat ability."

Lin Xuan gripped the Reverse Mortal Pearl.

"Kill an elder? An elder's strength is ten times stronger than a second-level demonic beast."

The woman nodded.

"I know. But you have the Reverse Mortal Pearl. The Reverse Mortal Pearl can reverse spiritual energy and reflect spells. You have a chance."

---

Lin Xuan remained silent.

Killing an elder came with a great cost.

But his right arm meridians were severed. Without treatment, they would be crippled forever.

Moreover, this was revenge.

Elder Zhang had participated in the Su family massacre. Killing him was revenge.

Su Qinghan looked at him.

"Lin Xuan, this is revenge. And it's also gratitude."

Lin Xuan looked at her.

"Gratitude?"

Su Qinghan nodded.

"The physician treating your severed meridians is a kindness. You helping her complete the mission is returning that gratitude."

She looked at the woman.

"Physician, are you certain Elder Zhang participated in the Su family massacre?"

The woman nodded.

"I personally saw Elder Zhang lead disciples to attack the Su family. He was one of the masterminds."

Su Qinghan said coldly.

"Good. Elder Zhang must die."

She looked at Lin Xuan.

"Lin Xuan, are you willing to help?"

---

Lin Xuan gripped the Reverse Mortal Pearl.

"I am willing."

The woman nodded.

"Very well. I'll first treat your severed meridians, then you complete the mission."

She approached Lin Xuan and placed both hands on his right arm.

"Reversing meridian severance, the cost is that your entire body's meridians will be reshaped. The process is extremely painful, but afterward, your meridians will be stronger than before."

Lin Xuan nodded.

"I accept the cost."

The woman began the treatment.

---

The process of reversing meridian severance was excruciatingly painful.

Lin Xuan felt a power flow into his right arm, reconnecting the severed meridians.

The power was cold, carrying a bloody scent.

The Reverse Mortal Pearl vibrated, dark red light flickering, resonating with the woman's healing power.

Lin Xuan felt his entire body's meridians being reshaped. The pain was so intense he nearly fainted.

But he didn't cry, didn't scream, didn't beg for mercy.

He simply endured the pain in silence.

---

An hour later, the treatment was complete.

Lin Xuan's right arm meridians were reconnected, and even stronger than before.

But his entire body's meridians had been reshaped and needed time to stabilize.

The woman looked at him.

"Meridian reshaping is complete. You now need three days of rest to stabilize your meridians."

Lin Xuan nodded.

"Thank you."

The woman shook her head.

"Don't thank me. You haven't completed the mission yet."

She looked at Su Qinghan.

"Qinghan, take Lin Xuan to the stone house to rest. In three days, he begins the mission."

Su Qinghan nodded.

"Alright."

---

The two left the medicine garden and returned to the outer sect stone house.

On the way, Su Qinghan looked at Lin Xuan.

"Lin Xuan, the physician's mission is very dangerous. Killing an elder could mean nine deaths in one life."

Lin Xuan nodded.

"I know."

Su Qinghan remained silent.

"Why did you agree?"

Lin Xuan looked at her.

"Because this is revenge. And it's also gratitude."

Su Qinghan looked at him.

"Revenge is for the Su family. Gratitude is for the physician. But you yourself may pay with your life."

Lin Xuan gripped the Reverse Mortal Pearl.

"I pay with my life because I choose to do so."

Su Qinghan remained silent.

She turned to look into the distance.

"Lin Xuan, I, Su Qinghan, depend on no one. But I recognize companions. You are willing to pay your life for revenge. I recognize you."

She turned and walked toward the stone house.

"In three days, I will accompany you to complete the mission."

---

Three days later, at the stone house.

Lin Xuan's meridians had stabilized, his right arm was normal again, and his strength had increased.

Su Qinghan arrived at the stone house.

"Ready?"

Lin Xuan nodded.

"Ready."

The two left the stone house and headed for the outer sect main hall.

---

At the outer sect main hall, Elder Zhang sat on a stone chair.

He appeared about fifty, with a cold face and sharp eyes. He wore a black robe, with a magical tool hanging at his waist.

Lin Xuan and Su Qinghan walked into the main hall.

Elder Zhang looked at the two of them.

"What are you here for?"

Su Qinghan said coldly.

"Elder Zhang, three hundred years ago, you participated in the Su family massacre."

Elder Zhang frowned.

"Su family? That matter from three hundred years ago, why bring it up?"

Su Qinghan continued.

"The Su family massacre, you were one of the masterminds. Today, I seek revenge."

Elder Zhang sneered.

"Revenge? You, an outer sect disciple, on what grounds do you seek revenge?"

He stood up, spiritual energy erupted.

Powerful spiritual energy pressure bore down on the two.

Lin Xuan felt the pressure, but the Reverse Mortal Pearl vibrated, dark red light flickering, reversing the spiritual energy pressure.

---

The Reverse Mortal Pearl's power reflected Elder Zhang's spiritual energy pressure.

Elder Zhang felt the reflected power and frowned.

"Reversing spiritual energy? You have the Reverse Mortal Pearl?"

Lin Xuan gripped the Reverse Mortal Pearl.

"Yes."

Elder Zhang sneered.

"The Reverse Mortal Pearl? That thing was buried by the Su family three hundred years ago. How did you obtain it?"

Su Qinghan said.

"The Reverse Mortal Pearl chose Lin Xuan. He inherited the power of reversal."

Elder Zhang sneered.

"Reversal power? The Su family's failed technique."

He waved his hand, launching a spiritual energy attack.

Powerful spiritual energy attacked the two.

---

Lin Xuan gripped the Reverse Mortal Pearl, and dark red light erupted.

The Reverse Mortal Pearl reversed the spiritual energy attack, reflecting it back at Elder Zhang.

Elder Zhang felt the reflected attack and was shocked.

"Reversing spells? The Reverse Mortal Pearl indeed has this ability."

He retreated, deploying spiritual energy defense.

But the reversed attack penetrated his defense and struck his chest.

Elder Zhang was injured, blood flowing out.

"Reversed attacks penetrating defense? The Reverse Mortal Pearl's power is stronger than the legends say."

---

The battle continued.

Elder Zhang launched spiritual energy attacks, and Lin Xuan reversed and reflected them.

But Elder Zhang's spiritual energy was too strong. The reversal could only weaken the attacks, not fully reflect them.

Lin Xuan felt the pressure. The Reverse Mortal Pearl vibrated, and the cost began to manifest.

The cost of reversing spiritual energy was that his meridians once again bore the pressure.

Lin Xuan's meridians ached, but he gritted his teeth and persisted.

Su Qinghan joined the battle, her ice-blue long sword slashing out, cold energy attacking Elder Zhang.

---

The two coordinated - Lin Xuan reversed spiritual energy while Su Qinghan attacked.

Elder Zhang, under attack from both, felt increasing pressure.

"Two outer sect disciples can actually fight an elder?"

He grew angry, and his spiritual energy eruption intensified.

Powerful spiritual energy pressed toward the two.

Lin Xuan felt the pressure. The Reverse Mortal Pearl vibrated more intensely, and the cost manifested.

His meridians bore the pressure again, the pain intense.

But Lin Xuan gritted his teeth, reversed the spiritual energy, and reflected the attack.

---

The battle lasted half an hour.

Elder Zhang's spiritual energy was depleted, his injuries worsening.

Lin Xuan and Su Qinghan were also injured, but they persisted.

Finally, Lin Xuan's reversed attack struck Elder Zhang's throat.

Elder Zhang fell, blood gushing out.

"The Reverse Mortal Pearl... indeed powerful..." Elder Zhang said weakly, then stopped breathing.

---

The battle ended.

Lin Xuan and Su Qinghan stood in the main hall, Elder Zhang's body on the ground.

Both were injured, but alive.

Su Qinghan looked at Elder Zhang's body.

"Revenge is complete."

Lin Xuan nodded.

"Yes."

---

Outside the main hall, disciples' shocked voices came.

"Elder Zhang has been killed!"

"It's Lin Xuan and Su Qinghan!"

"They killed an elder!"

Other elders arrived and saw Elder Zhang's body.

"Lin Xuan, Su Qinghan, you killed Elder Zhang?"

Lin Xuan nodded.

"Elder Zhang participated in the Su family massacre three hundred years ago. We sought revenge."

The elders remained silent.

"The Su family massacre... a matter from three hundred years ago..."

Another elder said.

"You killed an elder, violating sect rules. You must be punished."

Su Qinghan said coldly.

"Elder Zhang participated in the Su family massacre, this is fact. Our revenge is justice."

The elders remained silent.

---

At the medicine garden, the physician heard the news.

"Elder Zhang has been killed... revenge is complete..."

She sighed.

"The hatred from three hundred years ago is finally over."

She looked at the medicine garden.

"The Su family's hatred ends here. But new hatred may begin."

She looked into the distance.

"Lin Xuan and Su Qinghan killed an elder. The sect may retaliate. Their path will be even more difficult."

---

At the stone house, Lin Xuan and Su Qinghan rested.

Both were injured, but alive.

Su Qinghan looked at Lin Xuan.

"Lin Xuan, your right arm meridians are repaired, and even stronger."

Lin Xuan nodded.

"Yes. Reversing the meridian severance reshaped the meridians, making them stronger."

Su Qinghan looked at him.

"But you paid a cost. Your meridians bore the reversal pressure, the pain was intense."

Lin Xuan gripped the Reverse Mortal Pearl.

"The cost is paid, power is gained. The Reverse Mortal Pearl's power has grown stronger."

---

The Reverse Mortal Pearl vibrated, dark red light flickering.

More cracks appeared on the pearl's surface, as if telling Lin Xuan:

"Third cost preview: Reversing fate, a trial of life and death."

"Cost: Life-threatening danger, nine deaths in one life."

"Power gained: Reversing fate, changing the future."

Lin Xuan accepted in silence.

He didn't cry, didn't kneel, didn't beg for mercy.

The path of reversal continued forward.

---

Su Qinghan looked at him.

"Lin Xuan, you are willing to pay your life for revenge. I recognize you."

She turned and prepared to leave the stone house.

"Tomorrow, the sect may punish us. Be ready."

Lin Xuan nodded.

"I'm ready."

Su Qinghan left the stone house and disappeared into the night.

---

Late at night, Lin Xuan lay on the stone bed, his right arm meridians stronger, the Reverse Mortal Pearl's power greater.

He knew that tomorrow the sect might punish them.

But he didn't cry, didn't kneel, didn't beg for mercy.

This is the core of the Reverse Mortal Chronicles.
