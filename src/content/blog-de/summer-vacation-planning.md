## Summer Vacation Planning: From 5000 to 100K Budget

Summer vacation starting soon. How much budget? Where to go?

Dies ist nicht simple question. Different budget, different destination, different experience.

From 5000 yuan to 100,000 yuan, 5 budget tiers, 5 destination recommendations, complete itinerary planning, money-saving tips. After reading dies, your summer vacation perfect.

---

## Budget Tier 1: 5000 Yuan Southeast Asia Islands

### Destination Recommendations: Thailand Phuket or Vietnam Nha Trang

Why recommend?
- Short flight time (4-5 hours)
- Cheap prices (accommodation, food, entertainment)
- Good beach quality
- Many Chinese services

---

## Budget Tier 2: 10K Yuan Domestic Boutique Routes

### Destination Recommendations: Yunnan Dali Lijiang or Xinjiang Ili

Why recommend?
- Convenient domestic transportation
- Magnificent natural scenery
- Rich cultural experience
- High cost-effectiveness

---

## Budget Tier 3: 30K Yuan Europe Deep Tour

### Destination Recommendations: Italy or Spain

Why recommend?
- Deep cultural heritage
- Rich food experience
- Convenient transportation (train)
- High cost-effectiveness (Europe relatively cheap)

---

## Budget Tier 4: 50K Yuan Japan Full Tour

### Destination Recommendations: Japan Tokyo Osaka Kyoto Hokkaido

Why recommend?
- Convenient transportation (JR Pass)
- Diverse food
- Unique culture
- Rich experience (city + nature)

## Budget Tier 5: 100K Yuan Luxury Vacation

### Destination Recommendations: Maldives or Swiss Alps

Why recommend?
- World top vacation spots
- Ultimate service experience
- Unique environment
- Must-visit in lifetime
