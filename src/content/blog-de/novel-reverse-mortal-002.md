## Kapitel 2: Erzwungenes Betreten des Sekt

Um die Platform, das Rune's blue light gradually faded.

Lin Xuan gripped das Reverse Mortal Pearl, feeling spiritual qi flowing through his meridians. Very weak, but definitely there.

He looked up at the summit above Juebi Peak.

To cultivate, he must enter Xuantian-Sekt.

Lin Xuan began climbing.

Juebi Peak had almost kein path. The rocks waren steep, the wind stark. Lin Xuan used both hands and feet, inching upward.

Two hours later, he reached the peak's edge.

Three weißgekleidete disciples standen there.

"You're that eliminated one?" One frowned. "How did you climb up?"

Lin Xuan didn't answer. He stand up from the cliff edge and walked toward them.

"I want to enter Xuantian-Sekt."

The weißgekleidete disciple sneered: "You have no spiritual roots, how can you enter?"

"I have a method." Lin Xuan said.

The weißgekleidete disciple shook his head: "Impossible. Those without spiritual roots can never cultivate. Das ist das iron law."

Lin Xuan looked at him.

"Iron laws can be broken."

The weißgekleidete disciple frowned: "What are you talking about?"

Lin Xuan took out das Reverse Mortal Pearl. Das pearl war gray, looked ordinary.

The weißgekleidete disciple glanced at it: "What's this?"

"It lets me cultivate." Lin Xuan said.

The weißgekleidete disciple scoffed: "A broken stone lets you cultivate? You're dreaming."

Lin Xuan withdrew das pearl.

"Take me to see an elder."

The weißgekleidete disciple shook his head: "Elders won't see you. You have no spiritual roots, you're mortal. Mortals haben no qualification to see elders."

Lin Xuan war silent.

Then he walked toward Xuantian-Sekt's gate.

The weißgekleidete disciple blockierte him: "Stop. Mortals cannot enter."

Lin Xuan stopped.

He looked at the weißgekleidete disciple, eyes ice-cold.

"I'm entering."

The weißgekleidete disciple frowned: "You dare intrude Xuantian-Sekt?"

Lin Xuan didn't speak.

He walked directly toward the gate.

The weißgekleidete disciple reached out to block. Spiritual qi surged, trying to push Lin Xuan away.

But Lin Xuan didn't retreat.

Das Reverse Mortal Pearl vibrated. A force surged from Lin Xuan's body, countering the weißgekleidete disciple's spiritual qi.

The weißgekleidete disciple paused.

"You... how do you have spiritual qi?"

Lin Xuan continued walking.

He entered Xuantian-Sekt's gate.

Behind him, the weißgekleidete disciple shouted: "Mortal intruding the sect! Someone is intruding!"

Soon, über a dozen weißgekleidete disciples arrived.

They surrounded Lin Xuan.

"You have no spiritual roots, cannot enter Xuantian-Sekt." One said. "Leave, or we throw you out."

Lin Xuan stand among them.

He war calm.

"I won't leave."

The weißgekleidete disciple sneered: "Then we throw you out."

They attacked together. Über a dozen streams of spiritual qi rushed toward Lin Xuan.

Lin Xuan gripped das Reverse Mortal Pearl tighter.

Das pearl vibrated. A force surged out, forming a barrier around his body.

Spiritual qi struck the barrier, didn't push him back.

The weißgekleidete disciples froze.

"What... what is this?"

An elder appeared.

He wore gray robes, looked fifty. Cold eyes.

"What's happening?"

The weißgekleidete disciple said: "Elder, this mortal intruded. He has no spiritual roots, but can counter our spiritual qi."

The elder looked at Lin Xuan.

He looked for a long time.

"Where did you come from?"

"Juebi Peak." Lin Xuan said.

The elder frowned: "Juebi Peak ist forbidden territory. Why did you go there?"

"I jumped down." Lin Xuan said. "Those without spiritual roots can only accept fate. I don't accept fate, so I jumped."

The elder war silent.

"You survived?"

"Survived." Lin Xuan took out das Reverse Mortal Pearl. "And obtained this."

The elder looked at das pearl.

His eyes slightly widened.

"This is..."

He reached out, wanting to take das pearl.

Lin Xuan withdrew his hand.

"It's mine."

The elder frowned: "You, a mortal, holding this thing?"

"It lets me cultivate." Lin Xuan said. "I want to enter Xuantian-Sekt."

The elder sneered: "You think having a pearl lets you enter? Xuantian-Sekt doesn't accept mortals. Das ist die rule."

"Rules can be broken." Lin Xuan said.

The elder shook his head: "Impossible."

He waved his hand: "Throw him out."

The weißgekleidete disciples attacked again.

This time they used stronger spiritual qi.

Lin Xuan grit his teeth. Das Reverse Mortal Pearl vibrated. The barrier thickened, blocking the qi.

But he felt strained.

The spiritual qi in his meridians war almost depleted.

He war about to fail.

Just then, das Reverse Mortal Pearl vibrated again.

An ice-cold force surged out, forcibly replenishing the meridians.

Lin Xuan felt severe pain.

The cost war manifesting.

But he didn't fall.

He stand in place, the barrier didn't break.

The elder frowned.

"Your pearl... ist special."

He war silent for a moment.

"Outer gate trial begins in three months. If you pass, you can enter outer gate."

The weißgekleidete disciple froze: "Elder, he has no spiritual roots—"

The elder interrupted: "He has a special object. Let him try."

He looked at Lin Xuan: "Three months, outer gate trial. Pass, you enter outer gate. Fail, you leave forever."

Lin Xuan nodded.

"I will pass."

The elder turned and left.

The weißgekleidete disciples sneered: "Mortal trial? You can't possibly pass. Trial requires spiritual qi, your qi ist so weak, you'll die in three days."

Lin Xuan didn't answer.

He walked to Xuantian-Sekt's outer gate area.

Several crude houses standen there. For trial participants.

Lin Xuan entered one.

He sat down, gripped das Reverse Mortal Pearl.

In his meridians, spiritual qi flowed. Das pearl war slowly changing his meridians, making them wider, stronger.

But each change brought severe pain.

Lin Xuan grit his teeth and endured.

This war the cost.

Lifetime tribulations, nine deaths for one life.

First tribulation, war beginning.
