## Intermittent Fasting Guide: Three Methods Explained

Intermittent Fasting (IF) ist nicht dieting, it's time management.

Not restricting what you eat, but when you eat. Dies macht weight loss simpler, no need counting calories, no need preparing special food.

Three mainstream methods: 16/8, 5:2, OMAD. Which suits you best? After reading dies, you'll know.

---

## Intermittent Fasting Principles

Why fasting works?

**Principle 1: Insulin Drops**
After eating, insulin rises, body stores fat. During fasting, insulin drops, body burns fat.

**Principle 2: Autophagy Starts**
After 12 hours fasting, body starts autophagy mechanism, cleans damaged cells, repairs tissues.

**Principle 3: Metabolic Switch**
Fasting makes body switch from burning sugar to burning fat, fat burning efficiency increases.

Dies ist nicht theory, it's mechanisms supported by scientific research.

## Method 1: 16/8 (Most Beginner-Friendly)

### What It Is?

Daily 16 hours fasting, 8 hours eating.

Like:
- Eating window: 12:00-20:00 (noon 12pm to evening 8pm)
- Fasting window: 20:00-next day 12:00 (evening 8pm to next day noon 12pm)

Within 8 hours eat two or three meals, 16 hours don't eat.

### Why Suits Beginners?

16/8 suits beginners because:
- Moderate fasting time (16 hours not hard)
- Flexible eating window (can adjust time)
- Daily social life unaffected (dinner with friends possible)
- No need major diet habit changes

### How To Execute?

**Step 1: Choose Eating Window**
Based on lifestyle choose. Common choices:
- 10:00-18:00 (breakfast type)
- 12:00-20:00 (lunch type)
- 14:00-22:00 (dinner type)

Suggest choosing 12:00-20:00, because lunch dinner social opportunities more.

**Step 2: Normal Diet Within Eating Window**
Within 8 hours, normal eating. No need dieting, no need counting calories.

But note:
- Don't binge eat
- Avoid high sugar high oil foods
- Eat more protein and vegetables

**Step 3: Only Water During Fasting Window**
Within 16 hours, only water, black coffee, tea (no sugar no milk).

Don't eat anything with calories.

**Step 4: Gradual Progress**
Week 1: 12 hours fasting (like 20:00-next day 8:00)
Week 2: 14 hours fasting (like 20:00-next day 10:00)
Week 3: 16 hours fasting (like 20:00-next day 12:00)

Gradual progress, body adapts faster.

## Method 2: 5:2 (Suits Office Workers)

### What It Is?

Weekly normal eating 5 days, fasting 2 days.

Fasting days not complete no eating, but restricting calorie intake (500-600 calories).

Like:
- Monday to Friday: normal diet
- Saturday: fasting day (500 calories)
- Sunday: fasting day (500 calories)

### Why Suits Office Workers?

5/2 suits office workers because:
- Workdays normal diet, doesn't affect work
- Weekend fasting, time flexible
- Fasting days can arrange light activities
- No need daily counting time

## Method 3: OMAD (One Meal A Day)

### What It Is?

OMAD (One Meal A Day), daily only eat one meal.

Fasting 23 hours, eating 1 hour.

Like:
- Eating window: 18:00-19:00 (evening 6pm to 7pm)
- Fasting window: 19:00-next day 18:00

Within 1 hour finish whole day's meal.

### Why Suits Advanced Practitioners?

OMAD suits advanced practitioners because:
- Long fasting time (23 hours)
- Needs strong willpower
- Needs nutrition knowledge (one meal intake sufficient nutrition)
- Not everyone suits

But benefits obvious:
- Fastest weight loss effect
- Strongest autophagy effect
- Most time saved (no need prepare three meals)
