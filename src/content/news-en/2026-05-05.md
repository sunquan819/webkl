## Middle East

### Breakthrough in Iran Nuclear Negotiations

Iran and the US conducted their sixth round of nuclear talks in Geneva, reaching preliminary consensus on nuclear facility restrictions. Iran agreed to suspend uranium enrichment for 15 years, while the US pledged to lift some economic sanctions. This marks the closest point to an agreement in a decade, but whether it can be truly implemented depends on the sincerity of both parties. Easing Middle East tensions will impact global oil prices and geopolitical dynamics.

Saudi Arabia aims to stabilize oil revenue, but this may push up global energy costs. Production cut policies place significant economic pressure on developing nations.

Cooling inflation is a positive signal but still above the 2% target. The Fed's rate hike cycle may be nearing its end, but rapid rate cuts are unlikely.

## AI Regulation

### EU AI Act Takes Effect

The EU's Artificial Intelligence Act officially implemented, prohibiting AI for social scoring and real-time facial recognition. Tech companies must complete compliance by 2027. The world's strictest AI regulations balance innovation with risk. Chinese companies entering European markets face compliance challenges.

---

### China Releases New AI Regulations

China's Cyberspace Administration released the Artificial Intelligence Service Management Regulations, requiring AI companies to register, conduct data audits, and implement content filtering, effective July 1. China's AI regulations emphasize safety and control, differing from the EU's privacy-focused approach. Domestic AI companies must quickly adapt to the new rules.

The White House announced an international AI safety summit for June, inviting China, EU, Japan, and India to discuss limiting AI weaponization. International AI cooperation is the trend, but divergent national interests are evident. The US seeks to lead global AI rule-making.
